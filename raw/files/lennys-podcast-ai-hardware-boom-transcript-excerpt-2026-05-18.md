## Extracted Claims

- AI software work may eventually saturate behind-the-keyboard tasks, making the physical world the next frontier.
- Robotics and physical AI depend on supply chains for magnets, actuators, batteries, raw materials, and manufacturing expertise.
- VR may remain a niche, but VR investment produced spatial sensing, SLAM, depth, and human-perception knowledge that now transfers to robotics.
- AR glasses are a likely future interface, but mass production depends on display, waveguide, MicroLED yield, cost, and input challenges.
- Hardware differs from software because late changes are expensive and build cycles are limited.
- Humanoid robots are still advanced prototypes; safety, reliability, manufacturability, yield, and supply chain must improve before large-scale deployment.
- Robots near people require soft, compliant, non-threatening interaction design.
- Hardware teams need clear goals, early prioritization of hard constraints, and high iteration on the parts users touch most.
