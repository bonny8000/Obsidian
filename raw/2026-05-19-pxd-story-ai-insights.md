# Source 3: pxd story - Recent Design/AI Articles
**URL:** https://story.pxd.co.kr/
**Source:** pxd story

## Summary
A comprehensive repository of insights from pxd, a leading UX agency. The content covers the evolution of AI from simple tools to autonomous agents, the necessity of GEO for brand visibility in the AI era, and the practical application of AI in streamlining UX research and design systems.

## Key Insights
- **Agentic AI UX:** Shift from reactive prompts to autonomous agents. Requires "Harness Engineering"—designing the environment for agents.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Optimizing digital presence so AI recommends the brand.
- **AI in Research:** Tools like "Affinity Bubble" and "ReOpsAI" automate synthesis of user voices.
- **AX (AI Experience):** Journey of integrating AI into work and culture.
