## Summary

A DRS2026 (Edinburgh) research paper by Fengjin Yu and Qiaozhi Zhao (Loughborough University) that responds to the growing practical overlap between **Participatory Design (PD)** and **Co-Design**. The authors argue that existing scholarship is "largely confined to method comparisons" and lacks an **ontological** account of why the two converge. Their proposed move is a "fundamental ontological shift": they introduce **"generative events"** as the **basic ontological unit of design**, built by synthesising **Object-Oriented Design Ontology (OODO)** with **Process Ontology**. This reframes design as a dynamic generative process in which **humans and non-human objects collaboratively construct relationships and meaning within specific contexts**. The stated contribution is a unified theoretical foundation for the PD/Co-Design fusion and a methodological paradigm shift from a **"tool logic" to an "event logic."** The paper positions itself as developing **non-human-centred methods** and the **ontological deepening of "design as research."**
