## Summary (partial)

Ipsos frames **trust as "the ultimate wireframe"** for UX in the AI age: the field's central tension is the promise of AI speed versus the peril of eroding user trust. The piece is positioned as guidance for UX researchers on navigating that tension — treating trust as the underlying structure a good AI experience must be built on, the way a wireframe underlies a UI.

## Key Points (thesis confirmed; stats from related Ipsos work — verify against the report)

- Central tension: AI's promise of **speed** vs the peril of **losing user trust**; trust is the foundational layer ("wireframe") of AI UX.
- (Related Ipsos research) ~64% of Americans say products/services using AI make them nervous.
- (Related Ipsos research) Consumers trust humans and AI working **together** more than AI alone.
- (Related Ipsos research) Only ~11% of corporate communicators believe existing ethical policies suffice for widespread AI adoption.
- Ipsos framing: "while attention gets you noticed, trust is what makes you matter."
