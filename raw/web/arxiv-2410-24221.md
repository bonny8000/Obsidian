Subjects: Robotics; Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

## Collection Notes

- EgoMimic proposes using egocentric human videos with 3D hand tracking as scalable demonstration data for robot manipulation.
- The system combines human data capture, a low-cost bimanual manipulator, cross-domain alignment, and co-training on human and robot demonstrations.
- The paper argues that treating human and robot data as embodied demonstrations can improve long-horizon manipulation and generalization to new scenes.
- It reports a favorable scaling trend where additional human hand data can be more valuable than the same amount of robot data.
