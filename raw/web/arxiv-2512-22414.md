## Collection Notes

- This paper studies whether vision-language-action models can use human video data for robot skill transfer when trained at sufficient scale.
- The paper introduces a co-training recipe and argues that human-to-robot transfer emerges after diverse pretraining across scenes, tasks, and embodiments.
- It attributes the transfer effect to more embodiment-agnostic representations between human and robot data.
- The reported results suggest improved generalization in settings where the target skill appears only in human data.
