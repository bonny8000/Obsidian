## Collection Notes

- DreamDojo proposes a robot world model trained from large-scale egocentric human videos.
- It uses continuous latent actions as proxy actions to learn from unlabeled video interaction data.
- After post-training on smaller robot datasets, the model is positioned for simulating contact-rich robot tasks and supporting teleoperation, policy evaluation, and model-based planning.
- The paper also describes a distillation pipeline for faster real-time operation.
