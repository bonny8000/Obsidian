## Collection Notes

- This article summarizes research on when humans accept or reject a delivery robot boarding an elevator.
- Main themes: human-robot coexistence, elevator crowding, social acceptability, robot boarding decisions, and user experience in shared building infrastructure.
- It introduces Robot Boarding Area (RBA) as a way to reason about whether a robot has enough practical space to enter without requiring people to move.
- The article describes survey and VR-based experiments, with the practical implication that robots should consider crowding and social comfort, not only physical clearance.
