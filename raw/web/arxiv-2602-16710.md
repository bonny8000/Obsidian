## Collection Notes

- EgoScale studies large-scale egocentric human data for dexterous manipulation.
- The paper reports training a VLA model on more than 20,000 hours of action-labeled egocentric human video.
- It describes a two-stage transfer process: large-scale human pretraining followed by aligned human-robot mid-training.
- The paper reports better dexterous-hand performance than a no-pretraining baseline and suggests human motion can act as an embodiment-agnostic motor prior.
