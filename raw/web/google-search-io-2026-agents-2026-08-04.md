# Google Search at I/O 2026 — AI agents and more

## Announcements

### 1. Gemini 3.5 Flash becomes the default model in AI Mode
Global. Described as delivering *"sustained frontier performance for agents and coding."*

### 2. Redesigned "intelligent" search box
Claimed as the *"biggest upgrade to our Search box in over 25 years."* Dynamically expands for longer queries; AI-powered suggestions beyond autocomplete; accepts text, images, files, videos, and **Chrome tabs** as input. Rolling out immediately across all countries and languages where AI Mode exists.

### 3. Conversational follow-ups from AI Overviews
Follow-up questions flow directly out of an AI Overview with context carried across turns; links are described as becoming more relevant as the exploration deepens. Live on desktop and mobile worldwide.

### 4. Information Agents
Persistent background monitoring: *"Operating in the background, 24/7, these agents intelligently reason"* over blogs, news sites, social posts, and real-time finance, shopping, and sports data, then send synthesised, actionable updates. Example uses given: apartment-hunting alerts, notifications about a specific athlete.
**Availability:** summer 2026, Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

### 5. Expanded agentic booking — plus outbound calling
New categories: local experiences and home services, with live pricing, availability, and direct provider links. New capability: **Google will call businesses on the user's behalf** for home repair, beauty, and pet care.
**Availability:** United States, summer 2026.

### 6. Agentic coding and Generative UI in Search
Powered by **Google Antigravity** and Gemini 3.5 Flash. Search assembles bespoke responses containing interactive visuals, tables, graphs, and simulations — *"build custom layouts, assembling components in real-time."*
**Availability:** free to all Search users, summer 2026.

### 7. Custom dashboards and mini-apps
Persistent trackers for long-running tasks (a wedding, a house move, a fitness goal), integrating live reviews, maps, and local weather. Example: a fitness tracker with weekly progress.
**Availability:** coming months, Google AI Pro/Ultra US subscribers first.

### 8. Personal Intelligence expansion
Extended to nearly **200 countries and territories** in **98 languages**, with no subscription required for basic access. Connects Gmail and Google Photos; Google Calendar stated as coming. User controls every app connection.

## Caveats

- Information Agents, dashboards, and mini-apps are gated to paid tiers at launch; agentic booking and calling are US-only.
- Personal Intelligence requires explicit opt-in per connected app.
- Everything is described as "launching" or "rolling out"; nothing is universally available at the time of the post.
- No evaluation data, no accuracy figures, no failure-rate disclosure for any agentic capability — including outbound phone calls placed on a user's behalf.

## Interaction and ecosystem notes

- Users are being steered toward **longer, conversational queries** rather than keywords; the box expands to invite it.
- Multi-turn exchanges are designed to keep the user **inside Search** rather than leaving for a result.
- Ongoing monitoring is delegated to persistent background processes — a shift from pull to push.
- Generative UI and mini-apps substitute a generated surface for a visited page on some query classes.
- Information Agents read *"blogs, news sites and social posts"*; the post does not discuss compensation, attribution, or crawl consent for those sources.
- Agentic calling removes the web intermediary from service booking entirely.
