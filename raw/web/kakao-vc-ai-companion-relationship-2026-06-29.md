## Summary

A Kakao Ventures investment thesis arguing that AI services are moving beyond utility tools into **social roles** — friends, romantic partners, idols, role models — and that the **depth of relationship** an AI builds with users determines the **size of the business model** it can sustain. Core line: *"관계의 깊이를 설계하는 것이 곧 비즈니스 모델의 크기를 결정하는 시대"* ("an era in which designing the depth of the relationship directly determines the scale of the business model").

The post lays out a three-tier framework — **Agent-type (efficiency)**, **Attachment-type companion (relationship)**, and **Aspiration-type (identity/worldview)** — and argues that monetization potential, customer lock-in, and revenue breadth increase as you move up the tiers. It identifies **aspiration-type AI as the open/empty market** with the highest leverage, and frames AI VTubers (e.g., Neuro-sama) and IP/merchandise as the inflection point where relationship value converts into expanded revenue.

## Key Points

- **Thesis (verbatim Korean):** "관계의 깊이를 설계하는 것이 곧 비즈니스 모델의 크기를 결정하는 시대" — designing relationship depth determines business-model scale. AI is reframed from utility tool to social actor ("friends, romantic partners, idols, role models").

- **Tier 1 — Agent-type AI (efficiency-focused):**
  - Optimizes for performance: accuracy, speed, cost.
  - Trust comes from **functional capability**, not emotional connection.
  - Revenue = subscriptions with tiered usage limits.
  - **Structural weakness:** users defect immediately when a cheaper/faster service appears; comparison is easy and accumulated trust erodes fast from inaccurate responses.

- **Tier 2 — Attachment-type AI companions (relationship-focused):** splits into two sub-patterns:
  - **Quasi-romantic type:** users consume immediate excitement across **multiple characters simultaneously** (like dating sims) → shorter retention, lower lifetime value (LTV).
  - **Nurturing type:** users invest emotionally in **one character's long-term narrative arc** and feel co-growth → stronger emotional lock-in, higher retention, higher LTV.
  - **Offline analogy:** idol group **SHINee (샤이니)** — debuting young and sharing a long growth narrative with fans — is cited as the offline example of nurturing-type attachment.
  - **Scaling limit:** one-to-one chat architecture prevents community/shared-fandom experiences; monetization stays confined to chat-usage upgrades and customization options.

- **Inflection point — IP evolution via AI VTubers:**
  - **Neuro-sama**, an active AI VTuber, has **160,000+ (16만 명 이상) paid subscribers on Twitch** — cited as proof that community events and shared experience justify direct payment.
  - When individual characters become recognizable **IP**, revenue expands beyond platform subscriptions into **merchandise, advertising, IP licensing, events, and commerce channels.**
  - **Merchandise** is a signal of transition: buying branded goods = external identity expression and fanbase maturation beyond private consumption.

- **Tier 3 — Aspiration-type AI (identity/worldview-focused):**
  - If attachment is about **intimacy**, aspiration is about **emulation** — wanting to *resemble* the target.
  - Key claim: aspiration's essence "isn't fame magnitude but how intensely people desire resembling the target"; what matters is whether the entity **"consistently represents a particular belief system / worldview"** — human-vs-AI is not the deciding factor.
  - Cited offline aspiration targets: Korean artists **Lee Hyo-ri (이효리)** and **IU (아이유)** — artists with a clear conviction/attitude become objects of aspiration.
  - **Cultural-acceptance signal:** **Way of the Future** — a religious organization that venerates AI as a god, founded by former Google self-driving engineer **Anthony Levandowski** (the post notes its 2015-era origin) — cited as evidence AI is entering "belief and worldview" realms.
  - **Revenue implications:** consumers pay for **identity alignment / value adoption**; merchandise becomes "a symbol expressing what values I hold" and "a method of importing an aspirational attitude into my life"; communities become **shared-worldview spaces** where participants collectively emulate. Price resistance drops; lock-in strengthens.

- **Market gap / investment angle:** "The empty market is aspiration-type AI" — highest leverage: strongest customer lock-in + broadest revenue structure. Kakao Ventures frames the opportunity as **timing**: relationship-architecture design now determines future business scale, and companies building toward aspiration-type positioning control the largest future TAM.
