Sampling is the process of selecting units (e.g. people, organizations) from a population of interest so that by studying the sample we may fairly generalize our results back to the population from which they were chosen. Let’s begin by covering some of the [key terms in sampling](https://conjointly.com/kb/sampling-terminology/) like “population” and “sampling frame.” Then, because some types of sampling rely upon quantitative models, we’ll talk about some of the [statistical terms used in sampling](https://conjointly.com/kb/sampling-statistical-terms/). Finally, we’ll discuss the major distinction between [probability](https://conjointly.com/kb/probability-sampling/) and [Nonprobability](https://conjointly.com/kb/nonprobability-sampling/) sampling methods and work through the major types in each.
