## Summary

The post argues that demographic filters alone are not enough to identify real target users. It proposes designing recruiting criteria across three separate axes: inclusion criteria based on actual behavior, exclusion criteria for participants who may distort results, and diversity criteria that prevent one participant segment from dominating the sample.

## Extracted Claims

- Participant recruiting should distinguish behavioral inclusion criteria from demographic assumptions.
- Exclusion criteria are not simply the inverse of inclusion criteria; they remove people who qualify on paper but would distort the research.
- Diversity criteria should be planned as a matrix across behavior and demographic/contextual attributes.
- Poor recruiting can quietly distort analysis and downstream product decisions.
- Prior experiences shape mental models, so screening should ask what people have actually experienced or done.
