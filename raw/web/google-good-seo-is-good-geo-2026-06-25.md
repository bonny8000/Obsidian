# Good SEO is Good GEO — Brand Authority in the AI Search Era

**Author:** Brendon Kraham, VP of Search & Commerce, Global Ads Solutions, Google — Think with Google, June 2026.
**Capture status:** Fetched via web_fetch 2026-06-25; AI-written summary (not verbatim). Google's official position on optimizing for AI search.

## Summary

Google's thesis: **"good SEO is good GEO"** (also AEO / "AI SEO") — the new acronyms describe the *same foundational approach*, because AI features are built **directly on top of Google's core ranking systems** and retrieve current content from the **existing search index**. So the right move in the AI-search era is to double down on SEO fundamentals, not chase a separate "optimize-for-LLMs" playbook.

## Key Points

- **What changed:** Search hit an **all-time-high query volume** last quarter (cited: *Alphabet Earnings Release, Q1 2026*). AI Mode and AI Overviews let users discover, stay informed, shop, and complete tasks directly in Search. Generative features pull from the same index and core ranking systems as classic Search.
- **Five recommendations:**
  1. **Don't chase terminology.** GEO/AEO/LLM-SEO are the same foundational work; AI features build on core ranking. Don't bother with special formats like `LLMs.txt`.
  2. **Optimize for people, not bots.** Drop keyword-stuffing, artificial snippets, and inauthentic mentions — "Search is smarter than that."
  3. **Prioritize a unique perspective.** Authentic expertise and first-hand experience beat generic content. Example: a local running store analyzing *why a specific shoe collapsed* outperforms a generic "Top 10" listicle — non-commodity content is the competitive moat.
  4. **Build great web experiences.** Fundamentals: cross-device display, low latency, clear content hierarchy, relevant video/images.
  5. **Focus on business goals.** Measure real outcomes (leads, sales, signups), not third-party SEO scores — Google doesn't evaluate third-party tools and has no access to their internal metrics.
- **Tools for AI-era measurement:** Search Console's new **AI performance reports** (impressions from AI features); **Merchant Center** reporting on product listings in generative AI Search; **Google Business Profile** for visibility in AI responses.
- **Brand examples:** Lufthansa Group, Etsy, Royal Canin — cited as reimagining reach and campaign efficiency through AI Search marketing.
