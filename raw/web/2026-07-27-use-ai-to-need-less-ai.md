## Source summary

The article argues that teams should use AI to create durable specifications and contracts, then hand deterministic lookups to machinery that cannot improvise. Component specs, design tokens, and code contracts are presented as converging around a model where AI handles uncertain synthesis once, while tools and tests enforce repeatable facts thereafter.

## Reusable evidence

- Token values, component props, and Figma/code agreement are lookup or consistency problems rather than judgment problems.
- AI output becomes more reliable when it is grounded in explicit contracts and machine-checkable artifacts.
- The goal is not maximum model usage; it is reducing repeated model calls where deterministic systems are sufficient.

## Limits

This is a design-systems perspective and does not establish a universal boundary between judgment and lookup work.
