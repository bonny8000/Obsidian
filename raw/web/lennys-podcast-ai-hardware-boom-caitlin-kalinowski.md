## Extracted Claims

- AI hardware and robotics may be at an early inflection point, but deployment is constrained by physical supply chains, hardware components, and safety.
- Humanoid robots are framed as not yet ready for many mass-deployment use cases.
- Physical AI requires different product and engineering judgment than pure software AI because hardware systems face component, manufacturing, safety, and embodied interaction constraints.
- Consumer hardware lessons from Apple, Meta, and OpenAI may inform how AI devices and robots are designed and shipped.
