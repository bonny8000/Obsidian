## Collection Notes

- EgoBridge addresses the domain gap between egocentric human data and robot data for imitation learning.
- The method aligns policy latent spaces between human and robot domains using domain adaptation and an optimal-transport-based discrepancy measure.
- The paper reports a large success-rate improvement over human-augmented cross-embodiment baselines and claims generalization to objects, scenes, and tasks that are only present in human data.
- The arXiv page notes acceptance at NeurIPS 2025 and oral presentation at CoRL 2025.
