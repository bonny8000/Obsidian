# What Are the Different Types of Synthetic Users?

**Authors:** Jim Lewis, PhD & Jeff Sauro, PhD — MeasuringU, 2026-06-23.
**Capture status:** Fetched via web_fetch 2026-06-25; AI-written summary of the article's argument (not the verbatim text). The five-type taxonomy and the cited study are the article's own.

## Summary

"Synthetic users" is an umbrella **genus**, not a single species: it covers any AI-generated respondent meant to simulate human attitudes/behaviors in research. Lewis & Sauro propose a **preliminary taxonomy of five types**, ordered along a spectrum of *how grounded each is in real human data* — from ungrounded role-play to individual-level digital twins. They borrow biological-taxonomy framing (hierarchical genus/species) and explicitly flag the scheme as provisional and expected to evolve.

## Key Points

- **The five types (weakest → strongest grounding):**
  1. **AI Proto Persona** — weakest; generated from a simple role-play prompt ("You are a world-class Python programmer"). Preliminary profile from broad assumptions, no research backing.
  2. **Demographic Based** — uses demographic specs (age, gender, occupation, region) to approximate group tendencies; somewhat more grounded but still limited.
  3. **Persona Based** — richer persona descriptions (characteristics, behaviors, traits); more detail but still weakly grounded.
  4. **Research Grounded** — references actual research artifacts with traceable sources (interviews, surveys, analytics, support logs) without modeling individual responses; stronger grounding.
  5. **Digital Twins** — models individual-level data to replicate specific persons; strongest grounding, but real-world accuracy is still an open research question.
- **Organizing axis:** the single dimension that distinguishes the types is *grounding in real human data*. Detail/richness alone (e.g., a vivid persona) does not equal grounding.
- **Not the same as "generative agents."** Agents that model behavior *over time* are flagged as a qualitatively different category, outside this static taxonomy.
- **Hybrid approaches are emerging.** Real practice blends types; the authors cite **Bisbee et al. (2024)** as combining demographic + persona-based methods.
- **The taxonomy is provisional.** The authors expect classifications to change as the field matures.
