## Collection Notes

- EMMA proposes training mobile manipulation policies from egocentric human mobile manipulation data combined with static robot data.
- The method aims to reduce reliance on expensive mobile robot teleoperation.
- The paper reports performance comparable to teleoperated mobile robot data baselines across real-world tasks, with generalization to new spatial configurations and scenes.
