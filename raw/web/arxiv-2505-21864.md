Title: DexUMI: Using Human Hand as the Universal Manipulation Interface for Dexterous Manipulation

Authors: Mengda Xu, Han Zhang, Yifan Hou, Zhenjia Xu, Linxi Fan, Manuela Veloso, Shuran Song

Subjects: Robotics

DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.21864

## Collection Notes

- DexUMI uses the human hand as an interface for transferring dexterous manipulation skills to robot hands.
- It combines hardware adaptation through a wearable hand exoskeleton with software adaptation through robot-hand visual replacement/inpainting.
- The goal is to reduce embodiment and visual gaps between human hand demonstrations and robot hand execution.
- The paper reports strong real-world task success across two dexterous robot hand platforms.
