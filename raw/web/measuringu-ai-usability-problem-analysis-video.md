Title: How Reliable Is AI at Finding UI Problems?

## Collection Notes

- The article investigates whether AI systems produce consistent usability-problem lists when analyzing the same usability-test video multiple times.
- It evaluates ChatGPT-5.4 Thinking and Gemini 3 Flash Thinking with four runs each.
- The authors use any-2 agreement as a reliability measure and compare results to known evaluator-effect benchmarks.
- Reported summary: ChatGPT within-group reliability was fair, Gemini within-group reliability was good, and between-model reliability was low.
- The article clearly distinguishes reliability from accuracy/validity: consistency is a prerequisite, but does not prove that AI-discovered problems are correct or as relevant as human findings.

## Extracted Claims

- AI usability analysis should be evaluated for repeatability, not only plausible reasoning.
- Any-2 agreement can be used to measure agreement among problem lists.
- For the tested video/prompt, Gemini was more internally reliable than ChatGPT, while cross-model agreement remained low.
- UX researchers should avoid treating AI-generated problem lists as stable facts without validation.
