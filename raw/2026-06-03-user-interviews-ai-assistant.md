AI Recruitment

## Recruit faster with AI-assisted targeting & screening

With a simple prompt, our AI Assistant generates effective targeting criteria and screener questions for you, fully editable and review-ready.

#### Prompt-based targeting

Describe your ideal participants in natural language and instantly get precise recruitment criteria, ready to apply to your project draft.

#### Smart screener generation

Instantly generate screener questions with qualification criteria to refine your recruit, with full control to review and fine-tune before launch.

#### Project creation agent

Move from research idea to draft project through guided conversation. Our agent asks clarifying questions, shapes your plan, and creates a launch-ready draft in minutes.

AI Analysis

## Drive impact faster with AI insights and analysis

Move from raw data to actionable insights with [built-in analysis tools](https://www.userinterviews.com/insights): AI chat, session breakdowns, data grid, highlight clips, and more.

#### Instant debrief and analysis

Review session breakdowns for recordings, transcripts, and key observations, then compare participants side by side in the data grid.

#### Grounded insight exploration

Ask questions about your sessions with AI chat and get back cited answers. Every insight links directly to the exact moment in the transcript it is based on.

#### Flexible sharing and integration

Complete your analysis in User Interviews or continue in other tools. Share clips and reels with stakeholders, or export insights to bring into presentations, reports, or repositories.
