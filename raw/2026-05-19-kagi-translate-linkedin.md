## Summary
Kagi Translate now supports "LinkedIn Style" translation. AI can accurately replicate specific social/professional dialects, such as the "LinkedIn style," which is characterized by exaggerated positivity, professional jargon, and focus on personal growth. Translation is no longer just about language-to-language conversion but also "vibe-to-vibe" or "context-to-context" transformation.

## Key Insights
- AI can mimic specific online community tones (LinkedIn, Corporate Jargon).
- Translation shift: from linguistic accuracy to stylistic/contextual adaptation.
- Reframing personal updates into professional narratives.
