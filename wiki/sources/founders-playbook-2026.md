# Source: Founder's Playbook: Building an AI-Native Startup (2026)

- **URL:** https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6889473510b50328dbb70ae6/69fe2a55b93bb0732b1fe33c_The-Founders-Playbook-05062026_v3%20(1).pdf
- **Date:** 2026-05-06
- **Author:** Unknown (Startup Ecosystem)

## Summary
A technical and strategic guide for founders building startups in the AI-native era. It outlines a shift from "AI-added" to "AI-first" architectures, focusing on the **AI-Native Stack**, **Designing for Agency**, and the **10-Person Unicorn** organizational model.

## Extracted Claims
- **The AI-Native Stack:** Organizations must choose and manage three layers: Model (Proprietary vs Open), Infrastructure, and Application.
- **Designing for Agency:** Moving beyond chat interfaces to autonomous agents that perform tasks within the product.
- **The Feedback Loop:** Continuous model improvement via data flywheels is the primary competitive moat.
- **10-Person Unicorn:** Reaching scale with minimal headcount by using AI for both product delivery and operational "connective tissue."
- **Founders as Orchestrators:** Shifting from tactical execution to strategic generation and agent coordination.
