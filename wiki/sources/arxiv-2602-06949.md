# DreamDojo

## Citation

Gao, Shenyuan, et al. "DreamDojo: A Generalist Robot World Model from Large-Scale Human Videos." arXiv:2602.06949, submitted 2026-02-06.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06949

Raw source card: `raw/web/arxiv-2602-06949.md`

## Summary

DreamDojo proposes a [[concepts/robotics-spatial/robot-world-model|Robot World Model]] trained from large-scale egocentric human videos. It uses continuous latent actions as proxy actions for learning from unlabeled interaction videos and is positioned for simulation, teleoperation, policy evaluation, and model-based planning.

## Key Claims

- Large human video corpora can help pretrain robot world models for varied interactions.
- Latent proxy actions can address scarce action labels in human video data.
- World models can support planning and evaluation for contact-rich robotics tasks after robot-data post-training.
