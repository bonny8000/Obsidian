## Summary

The article argues that AI is making the distinction between procedural research work and wicked research work more visible. Procedural tasks are bounded, repeatable, and verifiable; AI is likely to absorb them fastest. Wicked work includes scoping, reframing, method-fit judgment, moderation judgment, and deciding when a clean answer would mislead. The article positions this wicked work as central to research seniority and harder to evaluate through artifact volume or studies shipped.

## Extracted Claims

- Research includes tame procedural work and wicked scoping/interpretive work.
- AI is strongest at the tame side of research once the problem is well framed and success criteria are specified.
- Scoping is wicked because stakeholder requests carry hidden history, incentives, and unstable framing.
- Preventing an unnecessary or misleading study can be high-value research work even when it leaves no conventional deliverable.
- Seniority should be judged by framing judgment and willingness to absorb social cost, not only by method accumulation.
