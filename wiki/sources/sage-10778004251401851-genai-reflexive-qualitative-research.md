# SAGE: Rejecting GenAI for Reflexive Qualitative Research

## Citation

Jowsey, Tanisha; Braun, Virginia; Clarke, Victoria; Lupton, Deborah; Fine, Michelle; and 414 signatories. "We Reject the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence for Reflexive Qualitative Research." Qualitative Inquiry, first published online 2025-12-17.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/10778004251401851

Raw source card: `raw/web/sage-10778004251401851-genai-reflexive-qualitative-research.md`

## Summary

This academic position article argues against using GenAI for reflexive qualitative approaches, including reflexive thematic analysis. It is a counterweight to AI research automation sources because it frames some forms of analysis as requiring human, positioned, reflexive interpretation.

## Key Claims

- Reflexive thematic analysis is not equivalent to coding or pattern extraction.
- GenAI may be methodologically incongruent with Big Q qualitative approaches.
- AI research workflows should be assessed through epistemic, ethical, environmental, and justice lenses, not only productivity.
