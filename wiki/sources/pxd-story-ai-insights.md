# pxd story: AI & UX Insights

## Citation
- **Title:** pxd story (Collection of AI & UX Insights)
- **Source:** pxd (UX Design Agency)
- **URL:** https://story.pxd.co.kr/
- **Date Observed:** 2026-05-19

## Summary
A collection of articles from the pxd design agency focusing on the evolution of AI UX. Key themes include the shift toward Agentic AI, the emergence of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and the automation of UX research synthesis.

## Extracted Claims
- The shift to Agentic AI requires a new UX paradigm focused on "Harness Engineering" [raw/2026-05-19-pxd-story-ai-insights.md].
- GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is becoming critical as users move from search engines to AI agents [raw/2026-05-19-pxd-story-ai-insights.md].
- AI is streamlining UX research by automating the synthesis of user voices (e.g., Affinity Diagrams) [raw/2026-05-19-pxd-story-ai-insights.md].
- AX (AI Experience) covers the integration of AI into both human work and organizational culture [raw/2026-05-19-pxd-story-ai-insights.md].
