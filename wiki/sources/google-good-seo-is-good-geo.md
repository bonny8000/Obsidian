## Summary

Google's official position on optimizing for AI-era Search: **"good SEO is good GEO"** (≈ AEO / "AI SEO"). The new acronyms describe the *same foundational work* because AI features (AI Mode, AI Overviews) are built **directly on top of Google's core ranking systems** and retrieve from the **same search index**. The takeaway: don't build a separate "optimize-for-LLMs" playbook — double down on durable SEO fundamentals and brand authority.

## Key Claims

- **AI features ride core ranking + the existing index** — so foundational SEO *is* generative-engine optimization. Don't chase GEO/AEO/LLM-SEO as distinct disciplines; skip special formats like `LLMs.txt`.
- **Optimize for people, not bots** — drop keyword-stuffing, artificial snippets, inauthentic mentions: "Search is smarter than that."
- **Unique perspective is the moat** — authentic expertise and first-hand experience (E-E-A-T-style) beat generic content; non-commodity content wins.
- **Web fundamentals still matter** — cross-device display, low latency, clear content hierarchy, relevant media.
- **Measure business goals, not third-party SEO scores** — track leads/sales/signups; Google doesn't evaluate third-party tools or see their internal metrics.
- **Search demand is rising, not falling** — an **all-time-high query volume** last quarter (cited: *Alphabet Earnings Release, Q1 2026*).

## Useful Examples

- **Local running store** analyzing *why a specific shoe collapsed* > a generic "Top 10 shoes" listicle — the unique-perspective / non-commodity argument made concrete.
- **AI-era measurement tools:** Search Console **AI performance reports** (impressions from AI features), **Merchant Center** generative-AI product reporting, **Google Business Profile** for AI-response visibility.
- **Brands cited:** Lufthansa Group, Etsy, Royal Canin.

## Constraints / Caveats

- **First-party (Google) source** — strong on Google's own surfaces, but inherently interested; it downplays standalone non-Google generative engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity) where retrieval/ranking differ.
- No methodology behind the brand examples; the "all-time-high queries" stat is an aggregate, not a per-site guarantee.
- Advice is directional, not a measured optimization study.

## Design Implications

- Refutes the "GEO is a brand-new game" framing inside [[concepts/product-management/geo-generative-engine-optimization|GEO]]: on Google surfaces, **invest in E-E-A-T + fundamentals + business-outcome measurement** rather than LLM-specific hacks.
- Counterpoint worth holding: non-Google answer engines may still reward distinct structuring — so "good SEO is good GEO" is Google-true but not universally proven.
- Connects brand authority to [[concepts/ai-agents/agentic-search|Agentic Search]] (what agents retrieve and cite).

## Tensions

- **"Nothing new, just do SEO" (Google) vs. "GEO is a new discipline" (much of the SEO industry + [[sources/pxd-story-ai-insights|pxd's GEO framing]]).** Likely both true on different surfaces: Google reuses core ranking; third-party LLMs don't.
- **Optimize-for-people vs. machine-readability** — Google says they converge; structured-data advocates partially disagree.
