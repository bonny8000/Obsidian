# Research Recommendations and the Roadmap

## Citation
- **Author:** Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g)
- **Date Added:** 2026-06-01
- **Location:** `raw/research-recommendations-roadmap.md`
- **URL:** [nngroup.com](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/research-recommendations-roadmap/)
- **Source Type:** Article

## Summary
The article provides 11 practical recommendations for UX researchers and designers to influence the product roadmap. It emphasizes that research impact is not just about the quality of the findings, but about timing, framing, and understanding the organizational context of product management. The shift is from being a "deliverable producer" to a "product decision shaper."

## Extracted Claims
- **Timing is everything:** Influence happens before the roadmap is finalized. Researchers must know the planning cycles and join discovery before solutions are defined.
- **Language of PMs:** Research must be translated into metrics that PMs are measured on (retention, conversion, etc.) to gain traction.
- **Findings $\neq$ Arguments:** Raw findings leave interpretation to others; clear recommendations tell stakeholders what to do with the data.
- **Usability Debt is Risk:** Framing usability and accessibility issues as "debt" or "risk" (legal, compliance, reach) is more effective than "it's the right thing to do."
- **Pre-meeting Legwork:** Socializing ideas with engineering and stakeholders before formal meetings reduces friction and increases feasibility.
