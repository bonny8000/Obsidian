## Summary

Ipsos frames **trust as "the ultimate wireframe"** for UX in the AI age: the central tension is AI's promise of speed versus the peril of eroding user trust, and trust is the foundational layer a good AI experience must be built on — the way a wireframe underlies a UI. Positioned as guidance for UX researchers navigating that tension.

## Key Claims

- **Trust is the foundational structure of AI UX** ("the ultimate wireframe"); speed without trust is a net loss. *(thesis — reliable)*
- Consumers trust humans + AI working together more than AI alone. *(related Ipsos research — verify)*
- A large share of consumers are nervous about AI-powered products (~64% of Americans, per related Ipsos work). *(verify against report)*
- Few corporate communicators believe current AI ethics policies are sufficient (~11%, per related Ipsos work). *(verify against report)*

## Design Implications

- Treat trust as a first-class, measurable UX outcome in AI features — design and research for it explicitly, not as an afterthought to speed.
- Favor human+AI collaboration framings over full-automation framings where user trust is at stake.
