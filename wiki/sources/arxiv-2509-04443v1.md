# EMMA

## Citation

Zhu, Lawrence Y., et al. "EMMA: Scaling Mobile Manipulation via Egocentric Human Data." arXiv:2509.04443v1, submitted 2025-09-04.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04443v1

Raw source card: `raw/web/arxiv-2509-04443v1.md`

## Summary

EMMA studies mobile manipulation policies trained from egocentric human mobile manipulation data and static robot data. Its goal is to reduce dependence on expensive mobile robot teleoperation.

## Extracted Claims

- Mobile robot teleoperation is a bottleneck for scaling mobile manipulation imitation learning.
- Human full-body mobile manipulation data can be co-trained with static robot data.
- The paper reports generalization to new spatial configurations and scenes.
