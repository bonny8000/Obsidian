# EgoBridge

## Citation

Punamiya, Ryan, et al. "EgoBridge: Domain Adaptation for Generalizable Imitation from Egocentric Human Data." arXiv:2509.19626, submitted 2025-09-23.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19626

Raw source card: `raw/web/arxiv-2509-19626.md`

## Summary

EgoBridge addresses the domain gap between egocentric human demonstrations and robot demonstrations. It aligns human and robot policy latent spaces using domain adaptation and an optimal-transport-based discrepancy measure.

## Extracted Claims

- Human-to-robot transfer is limited by visual, sensor, and kinematic gaps.
- Aligning policy latent spaces can preserve action-relevant information while improving transfer.
- The paper reports generalization to objects, scenes, and tasks seen only in human data.
