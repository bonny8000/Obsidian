## Summary

Newton argues UX research has long been defined by *methods*, and that definition is breaking because the **cost of producing research is collapsing toward zero** (AI now does transcription, coding, synthesis, tagging, recruitment, and first-pass analysis in an afternoon). When production cost collapses, the work moves up the value chain, splitting UXR into three roles: **Knowledge Makers** (create understanding), **Research Systems Builders** (create leverage), and **Orchestrators** (create impact). The throughline is a move "from methods to outcomes," with *interpretation* and *taste* becoming the scarce, valuable skills as information becomes abundant.

## Key Claims

- **Economic premise:** producing research is becoming near-free; a 2024 User Interviews survey found 90% of UX pros use AI in analysis/synthesis and 73% to summarize transcripts/notes. The work doesn't vanish — it moves up the value chain.
- **Knowledge Makers create understanding:** framing the right question, challenging assumptions, rigorous research/analysis, and the *taste* to recognize good vs bad output. AI can't produce knowledge alone; the role guards against "garbage in, garbage out." Information-gathering logistics evaporate; interpretation becomes scarce and valuable.
- **Research Systems Builders create leverage:** infrastructure that embodies researcher expertise (AI workflows, quality standards, automation, reusable knowledge) so anyone can self-serve trustworthy answers. The next expansion of ResearchOps — not "support." Likely consolidates today's researcher+ops collaboration into one role.
- **Orchestrators create impact:** combine signals from everywhere (studies, analytics, tickets, surveys, experiments, agents) into decisions; maintain product-team relationships; often *prevent* redundant studies ("you don't need one — we already know this"). Rarely produce research; ensure the org learns from what it already knows.
- **Thesis:** the real risk is not AI but defining UXR by commoditizing *activities*; reframe around outcomes. "This isn't researchers versus AI."

## Useful Examples

- **The three-role model itself** as a planning lens for UXR org design and career paths.
- **Birss "taste" point:** professionals' taste flags sub-par AI output instantly; AI makes novices *feel* competent, so spotting good/bad gets harder — an argument for why expert Knowledge Makers stay essential.
- **Orchestrator canonical move:** PM asks for a study → "You don't need one; we already know this from three sources — support cluster + experiment readout, answer by Thursday."
- **Systems Builder example:** a PM self-serves a themed answer from last quarter's study instead of commissioning a redundant one; a designer automates testing + trusted results.

## Constraints / Caveats

- **Practitioner thought-leadership essay**, not research — the three roles are a proposed model, not validated taxonomy.
- The framing is optimistic about AI doing the "mechanical" work well; it largely brackets the quality/΄hallucination risks the [[sources/guanjie-li-llm-user-proxy|LLM-user-proxy]] and [[sources/ada-kim-satisfaction-vs-benefit-ai|satisfaction-vs-benefit]] sources foreground.
- Cited stats (User Interviews 2024 survey; NN/g 2025) are referenced, not reproduced — verify before quoting precisely.

## Design Implications

- Plan UXR teams/careers around the three modes rather than method headcount: protect a **Knowledge Maker** core (taste + rigor), staff a **Systems Builder** capability (ResearchOps + automation), and grow **Orchestrators** as the decision-translation layer.
- The **Orchestrator** is the operational twin of [[concepts/ux-research/research-as-a-service|research-as-a-service]] — both put research close to decisions and legitimize "we already have enough evidence."
- Treat "taste" and interpretation as the durable differentiators when commissioning AI-assisted research (echoes [[concepts/product-management/product-taste|product taste]]).

## Tensions

- **Split vs convergence:** runs against [[concepts/product-management/role-convergence|role convergence]] (AI merging product roles) — research may *split* even as adjacent roles merge. Worth holding both as simultaneously true at different layers.
- **Democratized production vs quality:** near-free research production can flood orgs with low-quality findings; the Knowledge Maker's taste is the proposed safeguard, but staffing it is the open question.

## Open Questions
