# EgoMimic

## Citation

Kareer, Simar, et al. "EgoMimic: Scaling Imitation Learning via Egocentric Video." arXiv:2410.24221, submitted 2024-10-31.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24221

Raw source card: `raw/web/arxiv-2410-24221.md`

## Summary

EgoMimic studies how [[concepts/robotics-spatial/egocentric-human-data|Egocentric Human Data]] can scale [[concepts/robotics-spatial/robot-imitation-learning|Robot Imitation Learning]] for manipulation. It combines human video capture, 3D hand tracking, a low-cost bimanual manipulator, cross-domain alignment, and co-training on human and robot data.

## Extracted Claims

- Egocentric human video can be treated as embodied demonstration data, not only high-level intent.
- Co-training human and robot demonstrations can improve long-horizon manipulation.
- Additional human hand data may scale more favorably than additional robot data in the reported setting.
