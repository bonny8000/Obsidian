# Human-to-Robot Transfer in VLA Models

## Citation

Kareer, Simar, et al. "Emergence of Human to Robot Transfer in Vision-Language-Action Models." arXiv:2512.22414, submitted 2025-12-27.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22414

Raw source card: `raw/web/arxiv-2512-22414.md`

## Summary

This paper studies whether [[concepts/robotics-spatial/vision-language-action-model|Vision-Language-Action Model]] training can produce human-to-robot transfer when pretraining covers sufficient scenes, tasks, and embodiments.

## Extracted Claims

- Human video data may become useful for robot transfer at scale.
- Diverse robot pretraining can help produce embodiment-agnostic representations.
- Human-to-robot skill transfer is framed as an emergent capability under sufficient data diversity.
