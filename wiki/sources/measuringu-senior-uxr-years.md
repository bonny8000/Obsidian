## Summary

This article explores the professional requirements for achieving seniority in UX research by analyzing industry trends, salary surveys, and job postings. The research concludes that while there is no official rulebook, a five-year threshold serves as a reliable standard for the title. The study highlights that seniority is defined not just by technical skill mastery, but also by the ability to navigate complex corporate hierarchies and stakeholder politics.

## Extracted Claims

- The industry benchmark for a "Senior" UX Researcher title is consistently identified as at least five years of experience.
- Data from the 2024 UXPA Salary Survey shows that 86% of senior-level researchers have 5+ years of experience, with an average of 12–13 years.
- While agency experience can accelerate skill acquisition (e.g., conducting hundreds of sessions), it often lacks the corporate political and stakeholder management exposure found in in-house roles.
