## 🎯 Summary

Argues automated thematic summarization is the lowest-value use of AI in qualitative research. AI should be a "structured thinking partner" / co-consultant that pressure-tests human insights against evidence, while humans own interpretation and meaning-making — they hold context AI cannot access (observed moments, stakeholder dynamics, the unspoken).

## 💎 Key Claims

- AI-generated thematic summaries are low-value; reasoning support is the high-value use. (conf 0.8)
- Humans must retain ownership of interpretation; AI lacks situational context. (conf 0.85)
- Iterative human-AI collaboration beats hand-off automation in qual analysis. (conf 0.8)
