## Summary

Professor Yoo Hoon-sik outlines a paradigm shift where AI agents take over the manual labor of design, requiring designers to become high-level "architects" and "ethical controllers." The article emphasizes "Vibe Coding" as a way for designers to realize full-stack products through natural language and "Agentic Engineering" to manage complex AI workflows. Ultimately, the designer's value lies in their ability to curate aesthetic vision and build structured data assets that feed and guide AI systems.

## Extracted Claims

- The industry is shifting from passive "Generative AI" to "Agentic AI," which can autonomously plan, act, and observe to achieve strategic goals.
- Designers must transition from "execution-oriented" (pixel-pushing) to "decision-oriented" (governance and curation).
- Three essential future competencies are: **Vibe Coding** (natural language product creation), **Agentic Engineering** (building multi-agent systems), and **LLM Wiki** (constructing high-quality, cumulative design knowledge bases).
