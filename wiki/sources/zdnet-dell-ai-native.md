## Summary

At Dell Technologies World 2026, Vice Chairman Jeff Clarke argued that the survival of modern enterprises depends on a fundamental redesign of their operating structures to be AI-native. He introduced "Tokenomics" as a new economic reality where AI agents replace cognitive labor, drastically changing how companies calculate value and efficiency. Dell is partnering with Google Cloud to enable "air-gapped" on-premise AI environments, allowing regulated industries to run powerful models like Gemini on their own secure data.

## Key Claims

- Companies must move beyond simple AI adoption to becoming "AI-Native" organizations where the operating model is built around AI agents.
- The "Tokenomics" era has arrived: corporate costs are shifting from labor-centric to token-consumption-centric.
- Strategy shift: "Bring AI to the data" (On-premise/Edge AI) rather than moving data to the cloud, to ensure security and reduce latency.
