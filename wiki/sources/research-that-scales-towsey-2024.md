# Research That Scales: The Research Operations Handbook

- **Author:** Kate Towsey
- **Year:** 2024
- **Publisher:** Rosenfeld Media
- **Location:** `raw/research-that-scales-towsey-2024.pdf`

## Summary
A comprehensive guide to scaling research through systems thinking, rather than just increasing headcount. Towsey defines ResearchOps as the "power to act," focusing on delivering the right systems first and then administering them well. The book covers strategy, operational maturity, and the eight core elements of ResearchOps.

## Key Claims & Frameworks

### 1. Research Does Not Scale, Systems Do
Scaling research is not about producing "perfect clones" (like mass manufacturing) but about building a "Research Metropolis." It requires a systems-thinking approach where operations grow based on predictable and proportional inputs/outputs.

### 2. The Eight Elements of ResearchOps
Essential components for a scalable research practice:
1. **Participant Recruitment:** The biggest pain point; requires standardized cohorts and journeys.
2. **Knowledge Management:** Mastering organizational knowledge to scale value.
3. **Onboarding and Support:** Training, playbooks, and service desks.
4. **Tools and Vendors:** Compliance, utility, and interoperability.
5. **Ethics and Privacy:** Pervasive across all ResearchOps work.
6. **Money and Metrics:** Financial energy, savvy, and tracking ROI.
7. **Program Management:** Setting priorities straight and executing the right things.
8. **People and Skills:** Attracting, onboarding, and growing talent.

### 3. Phases of Operational Maturity
Systems progress through three phases:
- **Build/Buy and Standardize:** The "startup" phase, prototyping and learning what works.
- **Specialize and Optimize:** Tuning systems for higher quality and business value.
- **Keep the Lights On (KTLO):** Mastering ongoing administration without falling into complacency.

### 4. The RKM Model (Research Knowledge Management)
Distinguishes between:
- **Communities of Practice:** Decentralized, social, messy, focused on socialization and externalization ("campfires").
- **Knowledge Services:** Centrally managed, structured, focused on findability and trustworthiness ("libraries" and "repositories").

### 5. Respect in Research Trifecta
A synchronized approach to:
- **Ethics:** Caring for people before, during, and after research.
- **Data Governance:** Managing personal data throughout its lifecycle.
- **Data Privacy:** Ensuring compliance with laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).

### 6. PWDR (People Who Do Research)
A term coined by Towsey for non-researchers (designers, PMs, etc.) who conduct research as part of their role. This cohort requires specific "logistical support" and "craft support."
