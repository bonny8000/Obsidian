# EgoScale

## Citation

Zheng, Ruijie, et al. "EgoScale: Scaling Dexterous Manipulation with Diverse Egocentric Human Data." arXiv:2602.16710, submitted 2026-02-18.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16710

Raw source card: `raw/web/arxiv-2602-16710.md`

## Summary

EgoScale studies large-scale egocentric human data for [[concepts/robotics-spatial/dexterous-manipulation|Dexterous Manipulation]]. It trains a VLA model on a large action-labeled egocentric video corpus and uses a two-stage transfer recipe: human pretraining followed by aligned human-robot mid-training.

## Key Claims

- Large-scale human behavior data can be a scalable source for physical intelligence.
- Human video scale is reported to follow a log-linear relationship with validation loss in this setup.
- Human motion may provide an embodiment-agnostic motor prior for dexterous robot hands.
