# Kagi Translate: LinkedIn Style AI

## Citation

- **Title:** Kagi Translate now supports 'LinkedIn Style' translation
- **Source:** GeekNews (Original tool by Kagi)
- **URL:** https://news.hada.io/topic?id=27599
- **Date Observed:** 2026-05-19

## Summary

Kagi Translate has introduced a feature that allows users to translate text into specific "vibes," specifically "LinkedIn Style." This highlights a shift in AI translation from purely linguistic accuracy to stylistic and contextual adaptation, enabling the replication of community-specific dialects and professional jargon.

## Key Claims

- AI can accurately replicate specific social/professional dialects like "LinkedIn style" [raw/2026-05-19-kagi-translate-linkedin.md].
- Translation is evolving from "language-to-language" to "vibe-to-vibe" or "context-to-context" [raw/2026-05-19-kagi-translate-linkedin.md].
- AI can be used to reframe negative or mundane personal updates into overly positive professional narratives [raw/2026-05-19-kagi-translate-linkedin.md].
