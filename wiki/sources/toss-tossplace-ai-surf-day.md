## Summary

TossPlace treats AI as **organizational infrastructure** that reshapes work flows and business structure — not as a point productivity tool. The flagship practice is **AI Surf Day**: every Friday (Apr–Jun), employees leave Mon–Thu work to experiment with AI and apply it to real tasks. The motto (after Jon Kabat-Zinn): "You can't stop the waves, but you can learn to surf." The deliberate strategy is converting **individual discovery → organizational asset**.

## Key Claims

- **AI as infrastructure, not a tool** — the goal is workflow/business-structure redesign; productivity tips are a byproduct.
- **Protected exploration time is the precondition** — a recurring, calendared Friday (AI Surf Day) makes adoption happen rather than hoping for it after-hours.
- **AI Surf Club** — ~**200 self-organized groups** by role/interest (e.g., security engineers cataloging AI antipatterns; data engineers building shared knowledge wikis; a complaint-monitoring portal shipped in ~1 month; marketing teams making reusable AI assets).
- **AI Surf Weekly** — sessions sharing internal wins/lessons so teams reuse what peers prototyped (cross-silo transfer).
- **AI Surf Evangelists** — **142 employees** across subsidiaries chosen by **peer nomination** for enthusiasm (not technical seniority) to run workshops and champion adoption.
- **Culture beats program** — time allocation + psychological safety to share half-finished work + visible peer proof matter more than the formal structure. Participant quote: "The greatest gain wasn't improved AI skills but the confidence that 'we can do this.'"

## Useful Examples

- The **complaint-monitoring portal shipped in ~1 month** by a club team — discovery-to-asset in practice.
- **Peer-nominated evangelists (142)** — selecting for sharing behavior over technical rank.
- ~**200 self-organized clubs** as a model for bottom-up, interest-driven AI adoption.
