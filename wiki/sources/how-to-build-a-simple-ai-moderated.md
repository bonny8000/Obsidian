## Summary
A practical step-by-step guide for constructing a DIY conversational AI interviewer using Custom GPTs. Details system prompt structuring, probing techniques, and feedback capture mechanisms for qualitative user interviews.

## Key claims
- Custom GPTs can be configured as effective semi-structured interviewers when given explicit guardrails and follow-up rules.
- Adaptive probing prompts help extract rich qualitative insight compared to rigid forms or static surveys.

## Useful examples
- Prompt patterns for balancing active listening with topic redirection during automated participant interviews.

## Constraints / caveats
- LLM interviewers may suffer from acquiescence bias or hallucinate context if participant inputs are ambiguous.

## Design implications
- Incorporate clear exit conditions and human researcher review triggers for sensitive topics.

## Tensions
- Efficiency vs. Empathy: AI interviewers increase throughput but lack genuine human rapport.
