## Summary
MeasuringU 2026 quantitative UX benchmark evaluating usability metrics, task completion rates, SUPR-Q scores, and Net Promoter Scores (NPS) across major US healthcare provider and insurer websites.

## Key claims
- Website usability directly correlates with patient trust and brand NPS in digital healthcare touchpoints.
- Common usability friction points center on appointment scheduling, portal login navigation, and insurance coverage lookups.

## Useful examples
- Comparative SUPR-Q score breakdowns for healthcare portals vs. general consumer services.

## Constraints / caveats
- Benchmark data reflects US healthcare ecosystem expectations and regulatory constraints.

## Design implications
- Healthcare portals must prioritize simplified task paths for urgent information retrieval.

## Tensions
- Security/Compliance friction (MFA, hipaa disclosures) vs. UX simplicity.
