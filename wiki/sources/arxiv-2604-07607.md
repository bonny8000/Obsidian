# EgoVerse

## Citation

Punamiya, Ryan, et al. "EgoVerse: An Egocentric Human Dataset for Robot Learning from Around the World." arXiv:2604.07607, submitted 2026-04-08.

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07607

Raw source card: `raw/web/arxiv-2604-07607.md`

## Summary

EgoVerse introduces a collaborative platform and dataset for egocentric human data-driven robot learning. It emphasizes standard formats, manipulation-relevant annotations, shared protocols, and reproducible human-to-robot transfer evaluation.

## Key Claims

- Robot learning data collection remains expensive and difficult to scale.
- Egocentric human demonstrations can provide a scalable alternative if data collection and access are standardized.
- Human data helps policy performance, but alignment with robot learning objectives remains important.
