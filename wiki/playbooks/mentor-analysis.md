# Playbook: Mentor Analysis

## Purpose
A structured format to prepare for, document, and extract actionable insights from mentoring sessions or 1-on-1 meetings with senior stakeholders.

## Target Audience / Role
- UX Researchers
- Product Managers

## The Prompt / Template
```markdown
# Mentor Analysis: {{mentor_name}} - {{date}}

## 🎯 Pre-Meeting Goals
- What specific advice or decision am I seeking today?
- 

## 🗣️ Key Feedback & Directives
- What did they prioritize?
- What did they challenge or push back on?
- 

## 🧠 Underlying Assumptions (The "Why")
- What business metrics or strategic goals are driving their feedback?
- 

## ✅ Action Items
- [ ] 
```

## How to Use
1. Copy the template block before a 1-on-1.
2. Fill out the "Pre-Meeting Goals" to focus the conversation.
3. During or immediately after the meeting, capture the feedback.
4. Synthesize the "Underlying Assumptions" to better understand the stakeholder's perspective for future pitches.
