# Playbook: Weekly Report

## Purpose
This playbook standardizes the creation of weekly reports, ensuring consistent formatting, highlighting key achievements, and surfacing risks or blockers efficiently.

## Target Audience / Role
- UX Researchers
- Product Managers
- Designers

## The Prompt / Template
```markdown
# Weekly Report: {{date_range}}

## 🚀 Highlights & Achievements
- 

## 🚧 Work in Progress
- 

## ⚠️ Blockers & Risks
- 

## 🎯 Next Week's Priorities
- 
```

## How to Use
1. Copy the template block.
2. Replace `{{date_range}}` with the current week's dates.
3. Fill in the sections with bullet points summarizing your work. Use links to `[[projects/]]` or `[[decisions/]]` where applicable.
4. Share with the team or manager.
