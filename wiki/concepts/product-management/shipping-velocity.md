# Shipping Velocity

## Summary

Shipping velocity is the speed at which a team can move from idea to released product experience, then collect feedback and improve.

## Why It Matters

AI-native tools can compress development timelines, but faster shipping only helps if the team has a process for deciding what to ship, announcing it, supporting it, and learning from it.

## Key Claims

- AI product timelines can shrink from months to weeks or days.
- High velocity depends on removing launch friction across engineering, docs, marketing, and DevRel.
- Shipping faster can expose product inconsistency, especially when multiple experimental features overlap.
- Velocity should be paired with user feedback loops and quality checks, not treated as output volume alone.
