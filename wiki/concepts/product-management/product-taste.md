# Product Taste

## Summary

Product taste is the judgment to decide what should be built, which user problems matter, and what experience feels right when many possible features are technically buildable. The Figma taste framing adds a design-craft layer: taste is a cultivated point of view expressed through care, critique, trade-off judgment, and repeated intentional choices.

## Why It Matters

The transcript frames product taste as a rising bottleneck in AI product work. If AI makes code easier to produce, teams can build many things; the harder question becomes which things deserve to exist.

## Key Claims

- Code becoming cheaper increases the value of deciding what to write.
- Product taste helps teams choose among many user requests, GitHub issues, and internal ideas.
- Taste includes knowing the right UX for a model's current capability, not just imagining a future smarter model.
- Engineers, PMs, and designers can all contribute product taste if they understand users and quality.
- Taste is not replaced by AI because generated output still needs discernment, empathy, creative energy, and final judgment.
- In AI-assisted design, accepting the first output as final is a weak-taste failure mode.
