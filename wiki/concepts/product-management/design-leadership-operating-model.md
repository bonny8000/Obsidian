# Design Leadership Operating Model

## Summary

A design leadership operating model defines the balance between craft participation, coaching, delegation, decision quality, and business accountability.

## Why It Matters

“Be hands-on” is too vague to guide behavior. Teams need to know where a leader enters the work, what they own, and when they step back.

## Key Claims

- Leadership expectations should be expressed as observable behaviors and outcomes.
- Craft reviews are most useful when they unblock and teach rather than replace team ownership.
- The model should adapt to team maturity and decision risk.
