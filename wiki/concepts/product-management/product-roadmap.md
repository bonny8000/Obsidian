# Product Roadmap

## Summary
A product roadmap is a shared document that outlines the direction, priorities, and progress of a product over time. It can be organized around features, problems, or outcomes.

## Why it matters
The roadmap is the "source of truth" for what a team will build. Influencing the roadmap is the primary way UX research translates into shipped user value.

## Key Claims
- **Varied Structures:** Roadmaps can be feature-based (outputs) or problem/outcome-based (metrics). Research framing must match the roadmap's structure.
- **Planning Cycles:** Roadmaps are built during specific cycles (quarterly, biannually). Research must land *before* these cycles to be influential.
- **Prioritization Constraints:** Some items are immovable (mandates, deadlines), while others are "softer" and open to replacement by better-evidenced ideas.
