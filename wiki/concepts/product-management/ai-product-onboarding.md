# AI Product Onboarding

## Summary

AI product onboarding helps users discover core workflows, best practices, and feature changes in AI tools that evolve faster than users can track manually.

## Why It Matters

The transcript frames onboarding as a bigger need for agentic tools because features ship quickly and users can feel behind. Built-in education can reduce reliance on social media, scattered announcements, or trial and error.

## Key Claims

- Fast-changing AI products need mechanisms to teach users the most important workflows.
- Onboarding can conflict with the ideal that a product should be intuitive, but it becomes necessary when feature volume grows.
- A feature like `/power-up` is positioned as a way to surface core practices inside the product.
- Onboarding should guide users to reliable paths without turning into visible documentation overload.
