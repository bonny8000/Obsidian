# Compounding Knowledge

## Summary

Compounding knowledge means each new source improves the existing wiki instead of being treated as a one-off context window. The wiki becomes more useful as concepts, claims, and links accumulate.

## Why It Matters

This is the main distinction between an LLM Wiki and a simple chat upload workflow. The work spent understanding a source is preserved in Markdown and reused later.
