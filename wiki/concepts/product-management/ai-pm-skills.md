# AI PM Skills

## Summary

AI PM skills are the capabilities product managers need when building products around LLMs and agents: product taste, model literacy, eval design, user-task definition, launch judgment, and cross-functional coordination.

## Why It Matters

The transcript argues that AI changes which PM skills matter most. When implementation becomes cheaper, the scarce skill becomes knowing what to build, what quality means, and how to guide users through current model strengths and weaknesses.

## Key Claims

- Strong AI PMs understand the current model's limits, not just the future AGI vision.
- PMs should be able to define what successful behavior looks like through examples and evals.
- Product taste becomes more valuable as code generation becomes cheaper.
- Communication, stakeholder awareness, and common sense remain important because launches still have many human dependencies.
