# Stakeholder Management

## Summary
Stakeholder Management is the process of identifying, engaging, and influencing the individuals who have a stake in a product's success. For Senior UXRs and PMs, it is the bridge between research/product work and organizational impact.

## Key Primitives
- **Stakeholder Mapping:** Identifying who has the power to make decisions and who is impacted by them.
- **Conflict Resolution:** Navigating the differing priorities of engineering, marketing, legal, and finance.
- **Narrative Alignment:** Translating findings into a story that resonates with the specific goals of each stakeholder.

## Why it matters
Great work is often ignored if stakeholders don't trust the process or understand the value. Mastery of stakeholder management allows professionals to move from "deliverables" to "influence" (see [[concepts/product-management/research-influence|Research Influence]]), ensuring that their work actually changes the product's direction.
