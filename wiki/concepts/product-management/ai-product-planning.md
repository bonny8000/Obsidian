# AI Product Planning

## Summary

AI product planning uses AI to turn ideas, constraints, and business context into structured planning artifacts such as PRDs, functional specifications, user flows, and wireframes.

## Why It Matters

Planning artifacts sit upstream of design and code. If AI can structure them reliably, they can become a bridge from business intent to [[concepts/infrastructure-dev/design-to-code-workflow|Design-to-Code Workflow]] and [[concepts/ai-agents/planning-to-code-workflow|Planning-to-Code Workflow]].

## Key Claims

- AI planning tools can help non-specialists structure early product ideas.
- The output should be reviewable artifacts, not hidden chat context.
- Planning quality depends on clear assumptions, target users, constraints, and validation.
