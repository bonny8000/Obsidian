# Research Preview

## Summary

Research Preview is a launch pattern where a team releases an early feature with explicit expectations that it is experimental, may change, and may not be supported forever.

## Why It Matters

The transcript presents Research Preview as a way to reduce launch commitment and accelerate feedback. It is especially useful for AI features where model capability, UX, and user behavior are still moving.

## Key Claims

- Research Preview can lower internal friction by reducing the perceived cost of shipping.
- It helps users understand that a feature is early and feedback-oriented.
- The approach works best when paired with fast iteration and clear communication.
- It can create risk if users depend on experimental behavior without enough stability guidance.
