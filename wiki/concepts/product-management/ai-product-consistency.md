# AI Product Consistency

## Summary

AI product consistency is the coherence of product concepts, workflows, naming, and user expectations across many fast-shipping AI features.

## Why It Matters

The transcript identifies consistency as a cost of high shipping velocity. When many experimental features launch quickly, users may not know which path is best for a task or how overlapping features relate.

## Key Claims

- Fast AI shipping can create overlapping features and unclear product boundaries.
- Onboarding and education become more important when the product changes quickly.
- Research Preview can help set expectations, but it does not replace coherent product architecture.
- Consistency should be balanced against the learning value of shipping experiments.
