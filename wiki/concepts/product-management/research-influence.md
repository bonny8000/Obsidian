# Research Influence

## Summary
Research influence is the ability of UX research to shape product strategy, priorities, and the roadmap. It is distinct from research quality; high-quality research can have low influence if it is poorly timed, framed, or socialized.

## Why it matters
Without influence, research becomes a "footnote" in product development. Strategic influence ensures that user needs are built into the product roadmap rather than being treated as optional polish.

## Key Claims
- **Upstream Engagement:** Influence is highest during the discovery and framing phases, before solutions are committed to the roadmap.
- **Metric Alignment:** To influence decisions, research findings must be translated into the business metrics (KPIs) that stakeholders care about.
- **Recommendations over Findings:** Stakeholders often lack the time or expertise to interpret raw data; explicit, actionable recommendations increase the likelihood of adoption.
- **Relational Trust:** Influence is built over time through consistent, useful, and honest partnership with product and engineering teams.
