# Player-Coach Leadership

## Summary

Player-coach leadership combines enough hands-on craft fluency to unblock work with the responsibility to develop people and deliver outcomes through a team.

## Why It Matters

Leaders need credibility with the work without becoming the execution bottleneck or taking ownership away from the team.

## Key Claims

- Hands-on participation should be targeted at leverage points.
- Delegation and coaching are part of the role, not optional extras.
- The model needs explicit boundaries against micromanagement.
