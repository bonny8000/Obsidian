# Finance DA (Data Analytics)

## Summary
Finance DA (Data Analytics) is the application of data science and analytics techniques to financial operations, specifically for the purpose of automated reconciliation, risk management, and strategic financial planning.

## Key Primitives
- **Automated Reconciliation:** Using AI to match millions of transactions across different payment gateways and bank statements.
- **Predictive Modeling:** Forecasting future revenue and costs based on historical data patterns.
- **Financial Data Lake:** A centralized repository where financial data is normalized and linked to operational data (e.g., Nexus Data Lake).

## Why it matters
Traditional finance departments often rely on manual spreadsheets and periodic reports. Finance DA enables "real-time finance," where stakeholders can see the financial impact of product decisions immediately, leading to more agile and informed business strategies.
