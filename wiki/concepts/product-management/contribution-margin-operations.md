# Contribution Margin Operations

## Summary
Contribution Margin Operations involves managing product unit economics by tracking and optimizing the margin after variable costs (COGS, shipping, marketing, processing fees) are deducted from revenue.

## Key Primitives
- **Variable Cost Attribution:** Linking every operational expense to a specific transaction or user action.
- **Real-time Monitoring:** Using data lakes to track margin fluctuations as they happen.
- **Operational Levers:** Identifying which variable costs (e.g., shipping provider choice) can be adjusted to improve profitability without hurting UX.

## Why it matters
For high-volume startups, small improvements in contribution margin can be the difference between sustainability and burn. Integrating these metrics into the product's data infrastructure allows PMs to make data-driven decisions about pricing, promotions, and feature prioritization.
