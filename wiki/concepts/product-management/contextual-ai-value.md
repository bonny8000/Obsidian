# Contextual AI Value

## Summary

Contextual AI value is value created when an AI intervention uses the user’s immediate situation to improve a meaningful outcome, rather than merely increasing interaction with the AI feature.

## Why It Matters

The most visible feature metric may not be the user or business outcome that matters. Context can improve attention, relationship, or completion without increasing the targeted click or message rate.

## Key Claims

- Measure downstream outcomes alongside proximate behavior.
- Context should be sufficient, timely, and bounded.
- A monetization lift requires user-benefit and harm checks.
