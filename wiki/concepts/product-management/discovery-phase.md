# Discovery Phase

## Summary
The discovery phase is the early stage of product development focused on understanding user problems, needs, and opportunities before committing to specific solutions or features on a roadmap.

## Why it matters
Discovery is the highest-leverage time for UX research. Decisions made during discovery shape the entire trajectory of the roadmap.

## Key Claims
- **Problem Framing:** Discovery is about framing the right problem to solve, not just testing specs.
- **Early Influence:** Being involved in "messy" early conversations allows researchers to shape the roadmap before opinions are solidified.
- **Strategic Alignment:** Discovery helps ensure that the features being prioritized actually address real user pain points.
