# Tokenomics

## Summary

Tokenomics in the context of enterprise AI refers to a new economic reality where corporate costs and value creation shift from labor-centric models to token-consumption-centric models. It views AI tokens as the primary unit of cognitive labor.

## Why It Matters

As companies transition to being "AI-Native," the cost of operations becomes directly tied to the consumption of AI tokens by autonomous agents. This shift requires a fundamental redesign of business models and financial planning.

## Key Claims

- Corporate costs are shifting from traditional labor to token consumption.
- Tokenomics reflects the replacement of cognitive labor by AI agents.
- Enterprises must optimize their "token-to-value" ratio to remain competitive.
