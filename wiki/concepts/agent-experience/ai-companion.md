# AI Companion

## Summary

An AI companion is an AI positioned as a *social actor* — friend, romantic partner, idol, or role model — rather than a utility tool. The Kakao Ventures thesis frames companions on a three-tier ladder of relationship depth (Agent-type -> Attachment-type -> Aspiration-type), where each rung up trades easy substitutability for deeper lock-in and a broader revenue surface.

## Why It Matters

Companion AI is where AX (AI experience) stops being about task efficiency and becomes about relationship: retention, lifetime value, and price-resistance are set by how deep a bond the product sustains, not by accuracy or speed. That makes "relationship depth" a first-class product-strategy variable — and a user-safety concern.

## Key Claims

- **AI is shifting from utility tool to social actor** — "friends, romantic partners, idols, role models" — and relationship depth sets the ceiling on the business model ([[sources/kakao-vc-ai-companion-relationship|Kakao Ventures, 2026]]).
- **Three-tier ladder:** Agent-type (efficiency, functional trust, structurally fragile, sub-only) -> Attachment-type companion (emotional bond) -> Aspiration-type (identity/worldview, lowest price-resistance, strongest lock-in).
- **Attachment splits two ways:** quasi-romantic (many characters, immediate excitement, short retention, low LTV) vs nurturing (one character's long-term arc, co-growth, strong lock-in, high LTV).
- **Aspiration-type is "emulation, not intimacy"** — consumers pay for identity/worldview alignment; the human-vs-AI distinction becomes irrelevant.
- **The empty, highest-leverage market is aspiration-type AI** — strongest lock-in × broadest revenue (a deal thesis, not a validated taxonomy).

## Conflicts & Caveats

> [!warning] VC thesis, not empirical research
> The tier ladder is persuasive scaffolding for an investment argument, not a validated taxonomy. The only hard datum is Neuro-sama's 160,000+ Twitch paid subscribers; the source is a reconstructed translation of a Korean post and omits Western companion apps (Character.AI, Replika). Treat tiers as a planning hypothesis.
