# Companion Attachment & Dependency Risk

## Summary

The central tension of [[concepts/agent-experience/ai-companion|AI companion]] design: the engineered emotional/identity lock-in that drives retention and lifetime value is simultaneously a user-safety and dependency hazard. The same [[concepts/agent-experience/relationship-architecture|relationship-architecture]] levers that move a product up the business-model ladder are the ones that risk manufacturing unhealthy dependence.

## Why It Matters

The Kakao Ventures thesis treats "strongest customer lock-in" as the goal — but that phrase reads as a business win and a welfare risk at once. Without naming this tension explicitly, the companion-cluster concepts would describe only the monetization side of a mechanism that has a serious downside.

## Key Claims

- **Lock-in vs user welfare:** deep emotional/identity lock-in is the explicit design goal, and the very same mechanism is the dependency/manipulation risk in companion AI ([[sources/kakao-vc-ai-companion-relationship|Kakao Ventures, 2026]]).
- **Aspiration/worldview AI shades toward ideological capture:** monetizing "consistent belief representation" and building "shared-worldview emulation spaces" sits near persuasion/cult dynamics (the Way of the Future reference cuts both ways).
- **Authenticity gap:** nurturing "co-growth" is a designed narrative, not a real relationship — its value depends on users experiencing engineered intimacy as genuine.
- **Governance is unresolved:** as products are deliberately engineered for deeper lock-in, who sets and governs the safety/dependency boundary is an open question.
