# ? Orchestrator of Agents

The **Orchestrator of Agents** is the evolving role of a founder or team leader in an AI-native organization, shifting from "doing the work" to "designing the workflows" that AI agents execute.

## Key Attributes
- **Strategic Generation:** Focuses on high-order goal setting and architectural decisions.
- **Workflow Design:** Defining the sequence and interaction patterns between specialized agents.
- **Quality Control:** Setting the "bar" and verifying agentic output against business goals.
