# Conversational Canvas

## Summary

A conversational canvas is a design or creation workspace where the user directs an AI through conversation while the system updates a visual or interactive artifact.

## Why It Matters

It turns the interface of design work from direct manipulation only into a hybrid of prompting, editing, reviewing, and live artifact generation.

## Key Claims

- A conversational canvas can reduce tool-operation overhead.
- It may enable non-designers to produce early visual artifacts.
- Professional quality still depends on design critique and system constraints.
