# Gemini 3.5

## Summary

Gemini 3.5 is a frontier AI model family from Google DeepMind, specifically designed for "actionability" and autonomous agent workflows. It emphasizes "long-horizon" task execution, where the model can plan and execute multi-step processes autonomously.

## Why It Matters

The release of Gemini 3.5 marks a shift from conversational AI to "Agentic AI." Its "Flash" variant provides high speed and low cost for enterprise-scale agent deployments, enabling the automation of complex workflows that previously required significant human intervention.

## Key Claims

- Specifically designed for "long-horizon" tasks and autonomous agent workflows.
- Gemini 3.5 Flash outperforms 3.1 Pro in coding and agentic benchmarks.
- 4x faster and 50% cheaper than comparable frontier models.
- Integrated with the "Frontier Safety Framework" for ethical and secure agent operation.
