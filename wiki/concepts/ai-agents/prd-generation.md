# PRD Generation

## Summary

PRD generation uses AI to draft product requirements documents from prompts, source materials, user needs, business goals, and constraints.

## Why It Matters

A PRD can align product, design, and engineering teams. AI-generated PRDs are useful only if they expose assumptions and can be reviewed, revised, and connected to downstream artifacts.

## Key Claims

- AI can create a first draft of product goals, user problems, feature scope, and acceptance criteria.
- Generated PRDs should not replace product judgment.
- PRDs become more useful when linked to specs, flows, wireframes, and implementation tasks.
