# Product Evals

## Summary

Product evals are small, focused tests that define what successful AI product behavior looks like for real user tasks.

## Why It Matters

The transcript treats evals as a practical PM skill. Evals translate fuzzy product quality into examples, tasks, expected outcomes, and failure modes that engineers and model teams can use.

## Key Claims

- PMs do not always need hundreds of evals; a small set of strong evals can be useful.
- Evals are most valuable when a feature needs clearer behavioral definition.
- They help quantify whether a model/harness combination is improving.
- Features such as memory and agentic task completion benefit from explicit evals.
