# Amazon Bedrock

## Summary

Amazon Bedrock is AWS's managed service for accessing and orchestrating foundation models and agent capabilities. In this wiki, it appears as part of the enterprise AI infrastructure discussion around OpenAI models, Codex, and managed agents on AWS.

## Why It Matters

If coding agents and frontier models run through a cloud platform such as Bedrock, enterprise teams can route AI work through existing identity, security, logging, and procurement systems.

## Key Claims

- Bedrock is positioned as a unified environment for model access and governance.
- The collected Mashdigi pointer says OpenAI models and Codex are part of the Bedrock story, but this needs primary-source verification.
- Bedrock matters for design teams when AI workflows must operate inside enterprise security boundaries.
