# AI Inspection Bot

> [!abstract] Summary
> An automated agent designed to audit or "inspect" UI/UX elements against a defined set of heuristics or style guides.

> [!important] Why it Matters
> Enables continuous design quality assurance at scale, catching low-level errors before they reach production.

## 📝 Key Claims
- Can perform rapid audits of thousands of screens.
- Focuses human designers on higher-level strategic problems.
