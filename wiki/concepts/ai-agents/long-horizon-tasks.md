# Long-horizon Tasks

> [!abstract] Summary
> Complex tasks that require multiple steps, long-term planning, and the ability to maintain context over an extended period.

> [!important] Why it Matters
> Distinguishes next-generation AI agents from simple chat bots; enables autonomous problem-solving for entire projects.

## ?? Key Claims
- Requires advanced reasoning and "reasoning tokens" to plan several steps ahead.
- Context window size and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) are key enablers.
