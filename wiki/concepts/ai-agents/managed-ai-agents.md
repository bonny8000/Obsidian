# Managed AI Agents

## Summary

Managed AI agents are agent systems deployed through a platform that provides runtime, memory, permissions, logging, orchestration, and operational controls.

## Why It Matters

For production use, an agent needs more than model intelligence. It needs a reliable environment with identity, tool access, governance, compute, and observability.

## Key Claims

- Managed agents reduce the burden of assembling runtime components manually.
- Enterprise teams may prefer managed agents when sensitive data and compliance are involved.
- Product-specific claims require official documentation.
