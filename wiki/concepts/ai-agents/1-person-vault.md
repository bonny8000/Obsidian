# 1-Person Vault

## Summary
A personal knowledge management (PKM) system, typically built in Obsidian, that is operated and maintained by a single person to serve as their "digital twin" or external memory.

## Why it Matters
It represents a shift toward individuals owning and structuring their own data in a way that is ready for AI collaboration.

## Key Claims
- A vault can serve as the data backbone for personal projects, portfolios, and career reflection.
- It requires a balance between rigorous structure (ontology) and the ease of use necessary for a single operator.
