# AI Visual Collaboration

## Summary

AI visual collaboration is a workflow where humans and AI systems co-create visual artifacts such as prototypes, presentations, marketing materials, wireframes, or interface concepts.

## Why It Matters

It changes design collaboration from exchanging static files to iterating on shared, generated, and sometimes interactive artifacts.

## Key Claims

- AI visual tools can reduce the barrier to early visual communication.
- Structured artifacts are more valuable than one-off images when they can be edited, shared, and implemented.
- Collaboration quality depends on transparency, editability, and review.
