# Research Agents

> [!abstract] Summary
> Autonomous AI agents designed to execute user or market research studies end-to-end—spanning survey/test design, participant recruiting, data gathering, qualitative and quantitative analysis, and synthesis.

> [!important] Why it Matters
> Unlike simple LLM helpers that require human steering for each step, research agents assume ownership of the research pipeline. According to Qualtrics (2026), 84% of researchers who value AI for democratization believe research agents will run more than half of research projects end-to-end within three years.

## 📝 Key Claims
- **End-to-end task execution:** Research agents manage complex multi-step workflows (e.g., concept testing, transcript coding) rather than just single-task assistance.
- **Enables self-service insight democratization:** PMs or marketers can directly prompt a research agent to analyze qualitative findings or run basic usability checks without queuing tickets.
- **Reduces researcher overhead:** Frees up specialized researchers to focus on strategic methodologies and auditing instead of manual coordination.

## ⚖️ Conflicts & Caveats
> [!warning] Verification and Bias
> Relying on research agents end-to-end without human-in-the-loop audit gates runs major risks of "willful blindness" and methodological bias. If an agent misinterprets qualitative responses or hallucinates usability issues, decisions can be compromised. Agents must be bounded by deterministic validation rubrics.
