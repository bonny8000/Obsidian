# AI-Maintained Wiki

## Summary

An AI-maintained wiki delegates repetitive knowledge organization to an agent: summarizing sources, creating pages, linking related concepts, updating indexes, and flagging conflicts.

## Practical Contract

- Humans provide sources, goals, and review.
- The AI agent maintains `wiki/`.
- Original material stays in `raw/`.
- The instruction file governs how the agent behaves.
