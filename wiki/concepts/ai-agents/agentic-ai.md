# Agentic AI

## Summary

Agentic AI refers to a paradigm shift in artificial intelligence where systems move beyond passive generation (Generative AI) to autonomous action. These agents can plan, act, observe, and adapt to achieve complex, long-horizon strategic goals with minimal human intervention.

## Why It Matters

Agentic AI is viewed as the "next cycle" of AI development. It fundamentally changes the role of human professionals (e.g., designers becoming architects) and enables enterprises to automate entire business processes rather than just individual tasks.

## Key Claims

- Agentic AI autonomously plans and executes multi-step tasks.
- It enables a shift from "execution-oriented" work to "decision-oriented" governance.
- Requires high-quality, cumulative knowledge bases (like an [[concepts/infrastructure-dev/llm-wikiLLM Wiki]]) to guide autonomous decision-making.
- Specifically optimized for enterprise-scale "long-horizon" workflows.
- **Gemini Spark** serves as a core production example of an "always-on" agentic system integrated with everyday workspace tools. *(Brunch 497)*
