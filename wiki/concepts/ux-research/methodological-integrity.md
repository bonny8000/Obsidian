# Methodological Integrity

## Summary

Methodological integrity means the methods used in research fit the epistemology, aims, evidence, and claims of the study.

## Why It Matters

AI can make research faster while weakening the method if it performs work that the method requires humans to do. Conversely, AI can be useful when its role is aligned with the method and transparently validated.

## Key Claims

- Method fit matters more than tool novelty.
- Reliability and validity checks are part of integrity in usability analysis.
- Reflexive qualitative work may require human interpretation as part of the method itself. *(SAGE 2025)*
- Quality of AI integration depends on the researcher: rigorous input and interrogation of outputs is required; dumping raw data for AI to summarize creates false credibility. *(Lim 2026)*
- Replacing junior researchers with AI risks eroding the talent pipeline needed to use these tools with integrity. *(Lim 2026)*
- Method integrity starts before method selection: scoping must identify whether the team's request is a tame procedural problem or a wicked framing problem. *(Bakhshi 2026)*
- Integrity has an industry-facing test: evidence must support the claim, and the claim must answer the decision the organization actually faces. *(Bakhshi 2026)*
