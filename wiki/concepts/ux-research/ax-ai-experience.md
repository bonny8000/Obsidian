# AX (AI Experience)

## Summary
AX (AI Experience) is a specialized branch of UX design that focuses on the unique challenges and opportunities of interacting with AI-native systems. It goes beyond traditional interface design to consider the psychological and behavioral aspects of AI interaction.

## Core Pillars
- **Agency & Control:** How much autonomy the AI has versus the user.
- **Transparency & Trust:** How the AI explains its reasoning and limits.
- **Co-Creation:** How the user and AI collaborate on a shared task.
- **Proactivity:** How the AI initiates actions or suggestions without user prompts.

## Why it matters
Traditional UX principles (like predictability and consistency) are often challenged by the probabilistic nature of AI. AX design provides new patterns and mental models for creating AI products that feel helpful, reliable, and human-centric.
