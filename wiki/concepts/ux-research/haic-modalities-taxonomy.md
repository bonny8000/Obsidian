# HAIC Modalities Taxonomy

## Summary
The HAIC (Human-AI Interaction Components) Modalities Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing the different ways humans and AI systems exchange information. It was developed to standardize the design of AI-native interfaces.

## The Modalities
1. **Input Modalities:** How the user provides data (e.g., Natural Language, Sketching, Multimodal, Implicit Behavior).
2. **Interaction Modalities:** The flow of the dialogue (e.g., Turn-based, Streaming, Proactive, Ambient).
3. **Output Modalities:** How the AI presents information (e.g., Generative UI, Progressive Revelation, Multimodal Feedback).

## Why it matters
Standardizing these modalities helps design teams avoid reinventing the wheel for every AI feature. It provides a shared vocabulary for describing complex AI behaviors, from simple chatbots to proactive autonomous agents.
