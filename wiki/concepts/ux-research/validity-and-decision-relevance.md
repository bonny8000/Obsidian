# Validity and Decision Relevance

## Summary

Validity and decision relevance is a two-part accountability standard for industry research. Validity asks whether the evidence supports the claim being made. Decision relevance asks whether the claim answers the decision the organization actually needs to make.

## Why It Matters

AI can make research artifacts cheaper to produce, but artifact volume does not prove that the evidence is sound or that the work answers the right question. Research teams need both checks: a claim can be valid but irrelevant to the decision, and a claim can be decision-relevant but unsupported by the evidence.

## Key Claims

- Validity is a claim-support test: the available evidence must justify exactly the claim being made.
- Decision relevance is an action-fit test: the research must address the decision, constraint, or tradeoff the organization is actually facing.
- Industry research needs both standards because product teams act under time pressure and can be misled by persuasive but misdirected evidence.
- ResearchOps should operationalize the pair through intake standards, method-fit checks, evidence traces, and post-decision learning loops.
- AI-era research workflows should evaluate generated artifacts against both standards, not only against fluency, completeness, or stakeholder satisfaction.
