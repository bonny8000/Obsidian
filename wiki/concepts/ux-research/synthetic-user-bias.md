# Synthetic User Bias (Consensus Bias)

## Summary
The tendency of AI-generated synthetic users (powered by LLMs) to converge on statistical consensus, effectively smoothing out contradictions, minority opinions, and edge cases. 

## Key Claims
- Because LLMs are trained to predict the most likely response based on vast amounts of web data, they naturally gravitate toward the average. 
- In UX research, this means synthetic users will readily identify obvious usability issues but systematically ignore edge cases (e.g., accessibility needs, cognitive disabilities, non-native language struggles, or extreme emotional frustration).
- **Systemic Exclusion**: Synthetic user platforms inherit the biases of their underlying models, leading them to over-represent educated, tech-savvy, English-speaking demographics and under-represent marginalized groups.
- The output of synthetic users is often a "polished feedback loop" rather than messy, authentic human truth.
