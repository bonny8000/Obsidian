# UX Research Matrix

## Summary
A UX Research Matrix is a strategic tool used to map different research methods against two primary axes: **Attitudinal vs. Behavioral** and **Qualitative vs. Quantitative**.

## The Quadrants
1. **Qualitative/Behavioral:** Direct observation of user actions (e.g., Usability Testing, Contextual Inquiry). *Goal: Understand "How" and "Why" through action.*
2. **Qualitative/Attitudinal:** Understanding user feelings and beliefs (e.g., Focus Groups, Interviews). *Goal: Understand mental models and perceptions.*
3. **Quantitative/Behavioral:** Measuring user actions at scale (e.g., A/B Testing, Analytics, Eye Tracking). *Goal: Measure "What" and "How Many".*
4. **Quantitative/Attitudinal:** Measuring user beliefs at scale (e.g., Surveys, Card Sorting). *Goal: Identify trends and prevalence.*

## Why it matters
It helps research teams choose the right tool for the right question. Relying on only one quadrant (e.g., just surveys) creates structural blind spots in a product's user knowledge.
