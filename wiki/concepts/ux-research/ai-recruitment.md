# AI Recruitment

## Summary

AI Recruitment in UX Research refers to the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) and generative agents to automate and optimize the process of finding, screening, and targeting research participants.

## Why It Matters

Finding the right participants is often the biggest bottleneck in research. AI can reduce the time spent on manual screening and help researchers articulate complex targeting criteria more easily.

## Key Claims

- **Prompt-based targeting:** Natural language can be used to generate precise recruitment criteria.
- **Automated Screener Generation:** AI can draft screener questions and logic based on a research goal.
- **Conversational Project Setup:** Agents can guide researchers through the project creation phase to ensure high-quality recruitment drafts.
- **Selection Criteria Review:** AI-generated screeners still need explicit inclusion, exclusion, and diversity checks before they are used for real participant recruitment.
