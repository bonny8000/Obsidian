# Any-2 Agreement

## Summary

Any-2 agreement is a reliability measure that checks whether a usability problem identified in one evaluation appears in at least one other evaluation.

## Why It Matters

When evaluators, methods, or AI systems produce lists of usability problems, exact agreement is often low. Any-2 agreement provides a practical way to estimate overlap in problem discovery.

## Key Claims

- Any-2 agreement can compare AI runs with each other or compare different AI systems.
- It helps quantify repeatability of problem lists.
- It does not by itself establish whether the problems are valid or important.
