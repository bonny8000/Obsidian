# Ranking and Prioritization

## Summary

Ranking and prioritization convert comparative judgments into an ordered decision. The aggregation method is part of the evidence, not an invisible implementation detail.

## Why It Matters

Different methods can produce different winners. Teams should match the method to the decision, understand its assumptions, and preserve enough data to audit the result.

## Key Claims

- Force-ranking results are sensitive to aggregation rules.
- Borda, pairwise, and Schulze-style methods answer related but distinct questions.
- A ranking should include method, uncertainty, and validation notes.
