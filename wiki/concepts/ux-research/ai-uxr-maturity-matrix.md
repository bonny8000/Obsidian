# AI UXR Maturity Matrix

## Summary
The AI UXR Maturity Matrix, introduced in *The ResearchOps Review* (2026), uses a "Crawl, Walk, Run" framework to describe how organizations integrate Artificial Intelligence into their User Experience Research workflows. It maps the progression from ad-hoc task automation to systematic, agent-driven operations.

## The Three Phases

### Crawl
- **Focus:** Augmenting individual tasks.
- **Tools:** Off-the-shelf LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude) used manually.
- **Workflow Impact:** The traditional linear research workflow remains largely intact. AI acts as a "thinking partner" for drafting, summarizing, and brainstorming.
- **Visibility:** Gains are individual and largely unseen at the organizational level.

### Walk
- **Focus:** Team-level systems and purpose-centered agents.
- **Tools:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) repositories, custom GPTs, and specialized prompt pipelines.
- **Workflow Impact:** Steps in the workflow (especially data prep, analysis, and synthesis) begin to merge. AI is used for deeper analysis, and "evals" (AI evaluation) emerges as a necessary practice to manage quality.

### Run
- **Focus:** Production-grade, proactive research systems.
- **Tools:** Multi-agent pipelines, automated data ingestion (e.g., via APIs from data lakes), and Model Councils.
- **Workflow Impact:** The workflow condenses significantly. Research is delivered via Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) agentic systems that anticipate organizational needs and push insights to stakeholders before they ask.

## Why It Matters
Understanding AI maturity helps research leaders scope realistic operational goals. Moving from Crawl to Run requires shifting focus from *speeding up researchers* to *redesigning the operating system* (ResearchOps) to govern data, ensure methodological integrity, and manage "black box" risks.
