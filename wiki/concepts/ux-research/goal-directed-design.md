# Goal-Directed Design

## Summary
Goal-Directed Design (GDD) is a user experience design methodology developed by Alan Cooper. It focuses on identifying and designing for the specific goals of target users, rather than just listing features or tasks.

## Key Primitives
- **Personas:** Detailed, goal-based archetypes of users.
- **Goals:** The primary outcomes users want to achieve (Experience Goals, End Goals, Life Goals).
- **Scenarios:** Narrative-based stories of how a persona uses the product to achieve their goals.

## Why it matters
GDD ensures that product design is driven by high-level user outcomes. It helps prevent "feature creep" and "cognitive friction" by aligning the interface with the user's mental model and ultimate objectives.
