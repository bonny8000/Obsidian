# UXR Role Split (Knowledge Makers / Systems Builders / Orchestrators)

> [!abstract] Summary
> Kevin Newton's thesis that as AI drives the cost of *producing* research toward zero, UX research stops being defined by methods and splits into three distinct roles further up the value chain: **Knowledge Makers** (create understanding), **Research Systems Builders** (create leverage), and **Orchestrators** (create impact).

> [!important] Why it Matters
> The biggest risk to UXR is not AI but continuing to define the field by activities (interviews, surveys, transcription, synthesis) that are becoming commodities. Reframing the discipline around outcomes — understanding, leverage, decisions — is a planning lens for both careers and team design.

## 📝 Key Claims
- **Economic shift:** transcription, coding, synthesis, tagging, recruitment, first-pass analysis now take an afternoon, not weeks (a 2024 User Interviews survey: 90% of UX pros use AI in analysis/synthesis, 73% to summarize transcripts). When production cost collapses, the work moves up the value chain.
- **Knowledge Makers** create understanding: framing the right question, challenging assumptions, rigorous research, and the "taste" to recognize good vs bad output (AI can't produce knowledge alone; "garbage in, garbage out"). Interpretation becomes scarce and valuable as information becomes abundant.
- **Research Systems Builders** create leverage: the infrastructure (AI workflows, quality standards, automation) that embodies researcher expertise so anyone can self-serve trustworthy answers — the next expansion of ResearchOps, not "support."
- **Orchestrators** create impact: they combine signals from everywhere (studies, analytics, tickets, experiments, agents) into decisions, maintain product-team relationships, and often *prevent* redundant studies ("you don't need one — we already know this from three sources").
- The shift is "from methods to outcomes" — and is framed as the profession meeting the moment, not researchers-versus-AI.

## ⚖️ Conflicts & Caveats
> [!warning] Contradictions
> Sits in tension with [[concepts/product-management/role-convergence|role convergence]] (AI *merging* product roles): research may be *splitting* even as adjacent roles merge. Practitioner thesis, not empirical study — the three roles are a proposed model, and most orgs today blend them in one person. The Orchestrator role closely echoes [[concepts/ux-research/research-as-a-service|research-as-a-service]].
