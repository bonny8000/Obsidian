# Reflexive Thematic Analysis

## Summary

Reflexive thematic analysis is a qualitative research method where human researchers develop, interpret, and reflect on patterns of meaning in qualitative data.

## Why It Matters

The SAGE source argues that this method depends on subjectivity, reflexivity, and researcher positioning. That makes it a useful boundary case for evaluating where AI research automation may be inappropriate.

## Key Claims

- Reflexive thematic analysis is not merely categorization; researcher subjectivity and positionality are part of the method. *(SAGE 2025)*
- Replacing the interpretive work with GenAI may be methodologically incongruent. *(SAGE 2025)*
- Responsible AI use as a thinking partner can *intensify* reflexive practice by forcing clearer articulation of reasoning and exposing biases — it does not automatically undermine it. *(Lim 2026)*
- "AI doesn't replace reflexive practice — it makes it harder to get away with bad thinking." *(Lim 2026)*

## Conflicts / Caveats

SAGE and Lim disagree on whether any AI role is compatible with reflexive thematic analysis. SAGE argues methodological incongruence is inherent; Lim argues it depends on *how* AI is used (analyst vs. thinking partner). Both agree that AI-generated summaries are insufficient. Confidence held at 0.85 pending more practitioner evidence.
