# Operational Maturity Phases

## Summary
Research operating systems progress through three distinct phases of maturity. Understanding these phases helps in realistic scoping, investment planning, and hiring.

## The Three Phases
1. **Build/Buy and Standardize:** 
   - The "startup" phase driven by strategic priorities.
   - Focus on fast-paced experimentation, learning, and delivery of shiny new things.
   - Requires "change makers" and systems designers.
2. **Specialize and Optimize:**
   - Tuning and refining systems to meet more discrete and valuable priorities.
   - Finding efficiencies and optimizing partnerships.
   - Requires detail-oriented specialists.
3. **Keep the Lights On (KTLO):**
   - Mastering the ongoing administration needed to keep everything running.
   - Focusing on settling without slumbering (avoiding complacency).
   - Requires dependable people who enjoy known quantities and manageable workloads.

## Why It Matters
Teams often get stuck in the "Build/Buy" phase without planning for the long-term maintenance (KTLO) required to sustain operations. Mature operations should move from being driven by strategy to becoming an **informant** to strategy through the use of metrics and feedback.
