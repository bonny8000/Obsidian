# Physiological UX Research

## Summary
Physiological (or biometric) UX research involves measuring the user's physical response to an interface to understand their emotional state, cognitive load, or stress levels.

## Common Methods
- **Eye Tracking:** Measuring gaze points and fixations (where users look).
- **GSR (Galvanic Skin Response):** Measuring sweat gland activity to detect arousal/stress.
- **EEG (Electroencephalography):** Measuring brain activity to track cognitive load.
- **Facial Expression Analysis:** Using AI to categorize emotional responses (joy, frustration, surprise).

## Why it matters
Users are often poor at reporting their own emotional states or cognitive struggles. Biometric data provides an "objective" layer of evidence that bypasses the user's conscious filters, revealing exactly where an interface causes frustration or delight.
