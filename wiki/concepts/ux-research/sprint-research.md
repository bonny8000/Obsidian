# Sprint Research

## Summary
Sprint Research is the mid-tier operating mode in the [[wiki/concepts/ux-research/the-research-engine|Research Engine]], designed for moderate-risk and moderate-ambiguity questions, delivering findings in 1 to 2 weeks. It uses a two-layer approach: broad AI-moderated asynchronous data collection, followed by targeted, researcher-led live follow-up sessions.

## Why It Matters
Some questions are too complex for a 48-hour AI-only study but too urgent to wait for a 6-week foundational investigation. Sprint Research strikes a balance by using AI to achieve rapid breadth, allowing the human researcher to focus entirely on depth and nuance.

## Key Claims
- **Scope**: Used for multi-step workflows, new feature concept reactions, complex mental model mapping within a known domain.
- **Methodology**: 
  1. **Layer 1 (Breadth)**: 15-20 participants complete an AI-moderated asynchronous session.
  2. **Layer 2 (Depth)**: The researcher reviews Layer 1 data and selects 4-6 outliers or particularly rich/ambiguous participants for a short (10-20 min) live follow-up interview.
- **Analysis**: Combines automated pattern detection from Layer 1 with the deep contextual "why" extracted during Layer 2.
- **Common Pitfalls**: 
  - *Scope inflation*: Letting the study drift into Deep Research territory by adding too many sub-questions.
  - *Skipping follow-ups*: Treating it like a large Micro study out of time pressure, which wastes the opportunity for deep insight.
  - *Treating AI as a screener*: Failing to analyze the AI-moderated data as evidence in its own right.
