# AI-Native UX Design

## Summary

AI-native UX design treats AI agents, generated interfaces, adaptive workflows, and human-AI collaboration as first-class parts of the user experience.

## Why It Matters

The collected design sources frame UX work as moving beyond static screens toward systems where people interact with agents, generated artifacts, and conversational workspaces.

## Key Claims

- Designers may need to design the relationship between users and autonomous agents.
- AI-native design workflows combine research, prototyping, implementation, and validation.
- Product claims about specific tools should be verified against official documentation.
