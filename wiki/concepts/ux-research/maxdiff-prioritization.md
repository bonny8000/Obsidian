# MaxDiff Prioritization

## Summary
MaxDiff (Maximum Difference Scaling) is a survey-based research method used to prioritize a list of items (features, benefits, problems). Users are shown a subset of items and asked to pick the "best" and "worst" (or "most important" and "least important").

## How it works
By forcing users to choose extremes, MaxDiff eliminates the "straight-lining" and "everything is important" bias found in standard Likert scale ratings. The result is a ratio-scaled ranking of items that shows not just the order, but the relative distance between them.

## Why it matters
It provides a mathematically rigorous way to handle feature prioritization. For Product Managers, MaxDiff output is a "roadmap's best friend" because it clearly identifies what users value most and what can be safely deprioritized.
