# Design Research Automation

## Summary

Design research automation uses AI agents or tools to assist with research planning, secondary research, data organization, analysis, report generation, and insight synthesis.

## Why It Matters

Automation can remove research bottlenecks, but it also changes the burden of proof. Teams must distinguish AI-supported research operations from interpretive research claims.

## Key Claims

- Deep research agents can support market and user research workflows.
- Automated analysis should be validated through reliability and validity checks.
- Some interpretive qualitative methods may resist automation.
