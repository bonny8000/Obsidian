# Research Data Validation

## Summary

Research data validation is the practice of checking whether a tool’s outputs faithfully represent the raw responses and the intended analytic method.

## Why It Matters

Convenient dashboards can conceal scoring, filtering, weighting, or aggregation choices. Validation protects decisions from plausible-looking but incorrect summaries.

## Key Claims

- Preserve and inspect raw responses.
- Reproduce important outputs independently.
- Compare more than one defensible method when the result is method-sensitive.
