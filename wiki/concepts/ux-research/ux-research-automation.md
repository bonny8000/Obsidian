# UX Research Automation

## Summary

UX research automation uses AI or software systems to reduce repetitive work in research planning, data collection, synthesis, analysis, reporting, or usability review.

## Why It Matters

Automation can speed up research workflows, but research quality depends on method fit, interpretation, evidence, and accountability.

## Key Claims

- AI can support UX research by summarizing, structuring, and detecting candidate issues.
- Automated research analysis should be evaluated for reliability and validity.
- Reflexive or interpretive methods may require human analysis rather than AI replacement.

### Metrics & Performance Automation
> [!info] Insight from [[sources/tullis-albert-measuring-ux-2013|Tullis & Albert (2013)]]
> Automation in UX metrics isn't just about AI; it includes:
> - **Unmoderated Testing:** Using tools to automatically collect performance data (success, time, errors) and self-reported ratings.
> - **Physiological Automation:** Heart rate and skin conductance sensors for stress measurement.
> - **Data Cleanup Automation:** Essential for filtering 'noisy' data (e.g., extreme values/outliers) which AI can now assist with more intelligently.
