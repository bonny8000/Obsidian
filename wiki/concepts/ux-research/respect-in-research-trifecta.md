# Respect in Research Trifecta

## Summary
The Respect in Research Trifecta is a framework for ensuring that research is conducted with integrity and care for all involved. It consists of three synchronized parts: Ethics, Data Governance, and Data Privacy.

## The Three Parts
1. **Ethics:**
   - **Focus:** The human element.
   - **Goal:** Ensuring participants, researchers, and stakeholders are respected and cared for throughout the research process.
   - **Key Practice:** Informed consent (beyond just a form).
2. **Data Governance:**
   - **Focus:** The lifecycle of data.
   - **Goal:** Managing how personal data is collected, stored, transported, and archived.
   - **Key Practice:** Maintaining data integrity and enabling "Right to Be Forgotten" (RTBF) requests.
3. **Data Privacy:**
   - **Focus:** Legal compliance.
   - **Goal:** Adhering to privacy laws and regulations to mitigate legal and reputational risks.
   - **Key Practice:** Partnering with legal and security specialists early in the project.

## Why It Matters
A chain is only as strong as its weakest link. To deliver respectful research, all three parts must be addressed equally and synchronously.
