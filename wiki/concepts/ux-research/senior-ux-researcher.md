# Senior UX Researcher

## Summary

A Senior UX Researcher is a professional role characterized by mastery of technical research methods and the ability to navigate complex organizational dynamics. The industry standard for this title is generally a minimum of five years of relevant experience.

## Why It Matters

Defining seniority helps in career planning, hiring, and setting performance expectations. For Bonny's wiki, it establishes a benchmark for professional maturity in the UX research field.

## Key Claims

- Minimum of five years of experience is the consistent industry benchmark for the "Senior" title.
- Seniority involves both technical mastery and "corporate political" competence (stakeholder management).
- The average experience for senior-level researchers is actually closer to 12–13 years.
- Under AI automation, seniority becomes more visible in wicked work: framing judgment, method-fit judgment, moderation judgment, and willingness to absorb the social cost of pushback. *(Bakhshi 2026)*
