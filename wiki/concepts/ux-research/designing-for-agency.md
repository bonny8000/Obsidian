# 🤖 Designing for Agency

**Designing for Agency** is a product design philosophy that moves beyond chat-based interfaces (LLMs as "talking books") to agentic interfaces (LLMs as "doers").

## Core Principles
- **Task-Orientation:** The UI should help users define outcomes, which agents then execute.
- **Transparency & Trust:** Providing visibility into the agent's "thought process" and decision steps.
- **Intervention Points:** Allowing humans to "loop in" for critical decisions while letting the agent handle routine execution.
- **Tool Access:** Equipping agents with the necessary APIs and permissions to act on the user's behalf.

## Why it Matters
As highlighted in [[sources/founders-playbook-2026|The Founder's Playbook]], agency is what enables the [[concepts/product-management/10-person-unicorn|10-Person Unicorn]] by allowing a small team to manage complex, multi-step operations autonomously.
