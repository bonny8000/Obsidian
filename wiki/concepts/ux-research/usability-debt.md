# Usability Debt

## Summary
Usability debt is the accumulation of user experience issues, inconsistencies, and accessibility gaps in a product. Similar to technical debt, it accrues over time as features are added without addressing the core interface stability or user friction.

## Why it matters
Unaddressed usability debt eventually leads to a "wall" where building new features becomes twice as expensive because of the unstable or overly complex interface. It also increases support costs and reduces user satisfaction.

## Key Claims
- **Accumulation:** It accumulates quietly until it significantly hinders development velocity or user retention.
- **Risk Framing:** To get usability debt onto the roadmap, it should be framed as "risk" (legal, compliance, support volume) rather than just "design quality."
- **Invisibility:** Unlike features, usability improvements often don't have a specific customer request or sales push, meaning they need proactive advocacy from UX teams.
