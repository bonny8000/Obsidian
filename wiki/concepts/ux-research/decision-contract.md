# Decision Contract

## Summary
The Decision Contract is a mandatory scoping agreement established between the researcher and the stakeholder *before* a study begins. It is the primary quality gate for preventing scope creep and ensuring that fast research actually connects to a business outcome.

## Why It Matters
When research becomes faster and cheaper (via AI moderation), the volume of "nice to know" requests explodes. The Decision Contract forces stakeholders to articulate the utility of the research upfront. If they cannot fulfill the contract, the study is rejected or sequenced.

## Key Claims
A Decision Contract must define five things:
1. **The Decision**: What specific product decision will this research inform?
2. **The Owner**: Who is the named individual (not a team) accountable for acting on the results?
3. **The Outcomes**: If the study shows X, we do A. If it shows Y, we do B. What would change the team's mind? If the answer is "nothing", it is validation theater.
4. **The Boundaries**: What will this study *not* conclude? (e.g., "This 12-person comprehension study will not prove market fit"). This prevents stakeholders from overclaiming later.
5. **The Expiry**: When do these findings stop being valid? (e.g., "Valid until the pricing tier structure changes").
