# UX Career Progression

## Summary
UX Career Progression is the journey from junior-level execution to senior-level strategic leadership. It involves a shift in focus from mastering tools and methods to mastering organizational influence and product strategy.

## The Progression
1. **Junior/Associate:** Focus on tactical execution (e.g., running tests, wireframing).
2. **Mid-Level:** Focus on end-to-end ownership of features and improving process efficiency.
3. **Senior:** Focus on complex, cross-product problems and stakeholder influence.
4. **Staff/Principal/Director:** Focus on organizational strategy, research ops, and team leadership.

## Why it matters
Professionals who understand the progression can proactively seek the "stretch" opportunities (like [[concepts/product-management/research-influence|Research Influence]]) that are required for promotion, rather than just getting better at their current tactical tasks.
