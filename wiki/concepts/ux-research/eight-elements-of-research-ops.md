# Eight Elements of ResearchOps

## Summary
The Eight Elements of ResearchOps is a framework developed by Kate Towsey to define the core components of a scalable research practice. These elements are interconnected and interdependent, best visualized as a Venn diagram.

## The Eight Elements
1. **Participant Recruitment:** Standardizing how people are sourced, screened, and engaged for research.
2. **Knowledge Management:** Mastering the storage, findability, and synthesis of research insights (see [[concepts/ux-research/rkm-model|RKM Model]]).
3. **Onboarding and Support:** Providing the training and resources needed for people to use research systems.
4. **Tools and Vendors:** Managing the stack of specialized and generic tools used in the research workflow.
5. **Ethics and Privacy:** Ensuring that research is kind, honest, responsible, and legally compliant.
6. **Money and Metrics:** Administrating budgets and using metrics to tell the story of research impact.
7. **Program Management:** Executing the right research at the right time through prioritization frameworks.
8. **People and Skills:** Attracting, growing, and motivating research talent.

## Why It Matters
Failing to plan for the interconnectedness of these elements leads to under-specified programs and overwhelmed teams. To succeed, an initiative in one element (e.g., recruitment) must address all others (e.g., ethics, tools, metrics).
