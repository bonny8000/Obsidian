# Research as a Service

> [!abstract] Summary
> UX research operating as a professional *service* — judgment, standards, and responsibility for better outcomes embedded across the product lifecycle — rather than order-taking or appearing only when a formal study is requested.

> [!important] Why it Matters
> Reframes "service" (often heard as passive) as a route to *more* strategic research: by sitting close to decisions as they form, research shapes the questions teams ask instead of reacting to study requests.

## 📝 Key Claims
- Service ≠ order-taking; it is closer to servant leadership — helping the team make better decisions with fewer hidden assumptions.
- Research should start during project *shaping*: naming the riskiest assumptions before they harden into requirements.
- Pair closely with UX design/strategy as vague ideas become workflows and screens; help Product *see uncertainty* earlier.
- "We already have enough evidence to decide" is a legitimate, underused research contribution.
- The service mindset should not make the process heavier — it directs learning effort to where it matters, including post-launch.
