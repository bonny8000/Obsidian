# Reliability vs Validity

## Summary

Reliability is about consistency. Validity is about whether the result measures or identifies what it claims to measure or identify.

## Why It Matters

AI can produce confident UX findings that vary across runs or models. Measuring reliability helps determine whether results are stable, but stable results still need validity checks against evidence and human judgment.

## Key Claims

- Reliability is a prerequisite for trust, but it is not the same as accuracy.
- AI usability analysis should report consistency metrics before drawing broad conclusions.
- Validity requires checking whether identified issues are relevant and grounded in participant behavior.
- Validity should be treated as claim support: the evidence must justify the specific claim being made.
- In industry research, validity should be paired with decision relevance so teams do not act on true but misdirected evidence.

### Metrics Confidence
> [!quote] Tullis & Albert (2013)
> 'The statistics will work whether you have 2 or 100 users, your confidence level will change dramatically depending on the sample size.'
> - **Issue Detection:** Small samples (5-10) are often valid for finding high-severity problems.
> - **Performance Benchmarking:** Higher reliability (statistical confidence) requires larger sample sizes (10-20+ depending on the metric type).
