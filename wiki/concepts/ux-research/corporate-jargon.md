# Corporate Jargon

## Summary

**Corporate jargon** is the professional/community register ("LinkedIn style") that AI translation tools can now target as a *vibe*, not just a language. It is the canonical example of [[concepts/ux-research/contextual-translation|contextual translation]] — rewriting a casual or mundane message into the tone, hedging, and self-promotional framing expected in a professional setting, while preserving the underlying intent.

## Why It Matters

It shows translation moving from "language-to-language" to "register-to-register": the same content can be re-voiced for an audience. That is a double-edged UX affordance — it lowers the barrier to communicating in an unfamiliar professional context, but it also lets AI inflate mundane updates into "overly positive professional narratives," raising authenticity and trust questions.

## Key Claims

- AI can reliably reproduce a specific professional dialect (e.g. "LinkedIn style") as a translation target — a register, not a language.
- It can reframe negative or ordinary personal updates into polished professional narratives (a tone/authenticity risk).
