# Wicked Research Scoping

## Summary

Wicked research scoping is the senior research work of recognizing what question a team has actually brought, reframing it when necessary, and deciding whether a study should exist before a method is selected.

## Why It Matters

AI can make procedural research tasks cheaper, but it does not remove the need to decide whether the problem has been framed correctly. A clean study against an incoherent framing can produce a confident but misleading answer. Wicked scoping protects teams from measuring the wrong thing well.

## Key Claims

- Research contains both tame procedural tasks and wicked scoping tasks.
- Procedural tasks have clearer inputs, outputs, and checks; AI can accelerate them once the research question is stable.
- Wicked scoping has no clean stopping rule because stakeholder requests carry hidden assumptions, organizational history, and conflicting product intents.
- The value of scoping may appear as an absence: a misleading study was not run, or a feature question dissolved into a better product framing.
- Senior researchers earn trust by holding ambiguity long enough to see the real problem and by absorbing the social cost of challenging an initial request.
- Scoping protects decision relevance by checking whether the evidence a study could produce would answer the actual organizational decision.
