# Quant UXR Role Identity

## Summary
The role of the Quantitative UX Researcher (Quant UXR) is a specialized subset of user research that focuses on applying statistical methods, large-scale data analysis, and experimental design to product experience questions.

## Key Primitives
- **Statistical Literacy:** Beyond averages; understanding variance, confidence intervals, and significance.
- **Data Partnership:** Working closely with Data Scientists and PMs to triangulate big-data patterns with user-behavior signals.
- **Survey Science:** Designing rigorous instruments like MaxDiff, Conjoint, or longitudinal tracking.
- **Learning Pillars:** Building capability across statistics, surveys, programming, and research design.
- **Decision-Centered Rigor:** Treating rigor as appropriate learning for decisions, not merely advanced methods or large data sets.

## Why it matters
Quant UXR provides the "What" and "How Much" that complements the Qualitative "Why". In large organizations, the Quant UXR role is critical for justifying roadmap shifts and measuring the ROI of design changes at scale.
