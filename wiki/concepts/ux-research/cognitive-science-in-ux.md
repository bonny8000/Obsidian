# Cognitive Science in UX

## Summary
The application of cognitive science—the interdisciplinary study of the human mind encompassing psychology, AI, neuroscience, anthropology, philosophy, and linguistics—to User Experience (UX) research and design.

## Why It Matters
A frequent pitfall for UX professionals is restricting UX to surface-level usability testing and behavioral analytics. Integrating cognitive science ensures a deeper, more holistic understanding of user behavior, attention, cognitive load, and reasoning.

## Key Claims
- **Psychology** informs on heuristics, biases, emotions, and motivation.
- **Anthropology** highlights how society and culture shape the product experience.
- **Linguistics** affects UX writing, localization, and even perception (via the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis).
- **Neuroscience** reveals how biological factors (e.g., cortisol) influence interactions.
- **Philosophy** guides ethical and objective design decisions.
- **Artificial Intelligence** offers pathways to better UX through Expert Systems that model deep human expertise.
