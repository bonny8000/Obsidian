# Evaluator Effect

## Summary

The evaluator effect is the tendency for different evaluators to identify different usability problems from the same interface, task, or usability session.

## Why It Matters

AI usability analysis should be compared against known human evaluator variability. Low overlap does not automatically mean AI is useless, but it does require careful interpretation.

## Key Claims

- Usability problem discovery naturally varies across evaluators.
- AI systems may show within-system and between-system evaluator effects.
- Reliability metrics help distinguish acceptable variability from unstable output.
