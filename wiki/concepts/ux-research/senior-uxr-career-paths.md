# Senior UXR Career Paths

## Summary
As UX Researchers advance in their careers, they typically face a choice between two primary tracks: the **Individual Contributor (IC) track** and the **Management track**.

## The Tracks
1. **IC Track (Principal/Fellow):** Focuses on deep methodological expertise, complex cross-product research strategy, and high-level consulting for product leaders. *Goal: Master practitioner.*
2. **Management Track (Lead/Director/VP):** Focuses on building research teams, operationalizing research (ResearchOps), and securing budget/headcount. *Goal: Organizational leader.*

## Why it matters
Understanding these paths helps Senior UXRs proactively build the right skill sets—whether that's advanced statistical modeling and influence for the IC track, or coaching and stakeholder management for the Management track.
