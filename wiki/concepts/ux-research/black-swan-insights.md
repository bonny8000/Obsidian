# Black Swan Insights

## Summary
In the context of user research, "Black Swan" insights are highly unexpected, unpredictable, but extremely valuable user behaviors or feedback that fundamentally shift a team's understanding of the problem space. 

## Key Claims
- Real human participants frequently produce Black Swan insights by bringing their messy, out-of-context lived experiences, organizational politics, or physical constraints into a research session.
- AI-driven synthetic users struggle to generate these insights because they operate probabilistically. They produce *predicted* reactions based on existing patterns, not *accidental* or deeply contextual reactions.
- An over-reliance on automated research risks optimizing products solely for the expected, eliminating the serendipity of discovery.
