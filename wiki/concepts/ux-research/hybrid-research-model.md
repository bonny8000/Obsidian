# Hybrid Research Model

## Summary
A methodological approach that combines the speed and scale of AI synthetic users with the depth, authenticity, and edge-case discovery of real human research.

## Key Claims
- **Synthetic for Scale and Sanity Checks**: AI personas are best used for rapid, low-cost early validation, testing known hypotheses at scale, and identifying glaring "happy path" issues before investing in human research.
- **Humans for Truth and Emotion**: Real human participants remain essential for foundational discovery, uncovering physical friction, understanding socio-political organizational contexts, and generating [[wiki/concepts/ux-research/black-swan-insights|Black Swan Insights]].
- Responsible UX teams use AI to augment what they learn from humans, not to replace the labor of engaging with actual users.
