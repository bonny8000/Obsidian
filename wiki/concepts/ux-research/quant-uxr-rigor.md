# Quant UXR Rigor

## Summary

Quant UXR rigor is the discipline of using appropriate evidence to inform decisions under uncertainty; it is not simply advanced statistics, large samples, p-values, or rigid protocols.

## Why It Matters

Teams often treat "rigor" as a demand for more sophisticated methods or more data. The QuantUX framing is more useful for product work: rigor starts with the decision to be informed, then asks what data, method, stakeholder engagement, and strategic tradeoff are needed.

## Key Claims

- Method complexity is not the same as rigor.
- Statistical significance is not a general-purpose substitute for decision learning.
- Large data sets can amplify bad measurement or spurious associations if the research design is weak.
- Rigid protocols can protect comparability while reducing what the researcher learns from participants.
- A practical rigor stack moves through four levels: decision-focused learning, quality data and methods, effective stakeholder engagement, and higher-order strategic decision support.
- Rigor is strongest when paired with validity and decision relevance.
