# Robot Imitation Learning

## Summary

Robot imitation learning trains robot behavior from demonstrations. In the collected sources, demonstrations can come from robots, humans, egocentric video, teleoperation, or adapted human-hand interfaces.

## Why It Matters

Imitation learning is a practical route to complex manipulation, but collecting enough robot demonstrations is expensive. Human data is being explored as a scaling path.

## Key Claims

- Demonstration scale and diversity are central bottlenecks.
- Human data can complement robot data when aligned carefully.
- Embodiment and domain gaps determine whether human demonstrations transfer.
