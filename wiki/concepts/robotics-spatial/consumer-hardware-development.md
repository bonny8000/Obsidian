# Consumer Hardware Development

## Summary

Consumer hardware development focuses on physical devices for end users, where industrial design, engineering, manufacturing, cost, reliability, and user experience must converge.

## Why It Matters

AI devices and robots need consumer-hardware discipline if they are expected to operate safely, reliably, and acceptably in everyday environments.

## Key Claims

- Apple-style hardware excellence is framed as deep attention to every design decision, including details users may not directly see.
- Cost reduction can be a product strategy when the goal is broader adoption.
- Teams need clear product goals such as price, weight, performance, resolution, quality, and manufacturing yield.
