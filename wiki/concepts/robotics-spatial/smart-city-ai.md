# Smart City AI

## Summary

Smart City AI applies AI, robotics, digital twins, and spatial intelligence to city-scale infrastructure and services.

## Why It Matters

The city is a multi-layered UX environment: people, buildings, logistics, robots, vehicles, maps, and institutions all interact. AI must work across that full system to become useful infrastructure.

## Key Claims

- City AI depends on connected infrastructure, not only isolated robots.
- Building-scale testbeds can become prototypes for city-scale deployment.
- Last-mile movement and indoor/outdoor continuity are important smart-city concerns.
