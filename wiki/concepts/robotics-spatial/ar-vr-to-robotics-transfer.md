# AR/VR to Robotics Transfer

## Summary

AR/VR to Robotics Transfer is the idea that technologies developed for virtual and augmented reality, such as spatial tracking, SLAM, depth sensing, and human perception modeling, can become foundations for robotics and physical AI.

## Why It Matters

Even if consumer VR remains niche, its technical investment can still compound into robotics. Robots need to localize, perceive depth, reason about space, and sometimes be teleoperated through immersive interfaces.

## Key Claims

- VR helped teams understand how to orient digital and real-world objects in space.
- SLAM, depth applications, and spatial perception are relevant to robot navigation and control.
- AR/VR hardware programs may create talent, components, and sensing expertise for physical AI systems.
