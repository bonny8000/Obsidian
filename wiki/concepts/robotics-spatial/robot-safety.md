# Robot Safety

## Summary

Robot safety covers the physical, software, adversarial, and interaction safeguards needed when robots operate around people or in shared infrastructure.

## Why It Matters

Robots can cause physical harm or social disruption if they misperceive, move unsafely, leak data, or respond incorrectly to adversarial instructions. Safety must include both embodied mechanics and AI behavior.

## Key Claims

- Physical design choices such as softness, mass distribution, compliance, and speed affect human safety.
- AI safety risks become more serious when model outputs control physical movement or sensors.
- Social acceptability is also a safety-adjacent concern in shared spaces such as elevators.
- Security and adversarial threats to hardware layers need explicit attention.
