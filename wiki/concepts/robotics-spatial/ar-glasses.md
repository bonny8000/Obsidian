# AR Glasses

## Summary

AR glasses are wearable displays that overlay digital information into a user's field of view while preserving some social connection with the physical world.

## Why It Matters

The podcast frames AR glasses as a likely future interface but not yet ready for mass production at the desired quality, field of view, cost, and input model.

## Key Claims

- AR glasses may reduce the need to look down at phones while staying socially present.
- Prototypes such as wide-field-of-view AR glasses can reveal future interface potential before mass production is ready.
- Key blockers include waveguides, MicroLED yield, cost, and quiet/public input methods.
