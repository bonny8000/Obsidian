# Hardware Product Development

## Summary

Hardware product development is the process of designing, testing, manufacturing, and shipping physical products where late changes are expensive and iteration cycles are limited.

## Why It Matters

AI hardware and robots cannot be patched like software. Mechanical, electrical, supply-chain, manufacturing, and reliability constraints must be handled before mass production.

## Key Claims

- Hardware has only a few major "compile" cycles before mass production.
- Teams should define goals early and change them cautiously.
- The riskiest or hardest physical constraints should be designed first.
- Components users touch or interact with most deserve extra iteration.
- Known work should be done immediately because hardware schedules absorb surprises.
