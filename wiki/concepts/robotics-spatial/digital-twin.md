# Digital Twin

## Summary

A digital twin is a digital model of a physical environment, object, building, or city that can be used for simulation, monitoring, navigation, planning, and service operations.

## Why It Matters

For robots and smart cities, digital twins provide the shared spatial substrate that links perception, planning, physical infrastructure, and service workflows.

## Key Claims

- Digital twins are part of the spatial AI stack described by NAVER LABS.
- They become more valuable when continuously updated with real-world data.
- For city-scale use, digital twins connect indoor spaces, logistics, roads, and services.
