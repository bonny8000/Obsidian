# Immersion vs Presence

## Summary
In spatial computing and VR/AR research, **Immersion** and **Presence** are two distinct but related concepts. 

## The Difference
- **Immersion (Technical):** The objective level of sensory fidelity provided by a system (e.g., FOV, resolution, tracking accuracy). It is a property of the technology.
- **Presence (Psychological):** The subjective "feeling of being there" in the virtual environment. It is a property of the user's consciousness.

## Why it matters
High immersion does not always guarantee high presence, and vice-versa. For robotics teleoperation or VR training, the goal is often to maximize presence so the user's skills and reactions transfer naturally to the virtual or remote environment.
