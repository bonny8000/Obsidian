# Visual Localization

## Summary

Visual localization estimates a camera or robot's position and orientation from visual input, often by matching the current view against a map or learned spatial representation.

## Why It Matters

Robots in buildings need reliable localization where GPS is unavailable or unreliable. Visual localization is one way to connect camera perception with spatial maps.

## Key Claims

- A robot's ability to act in a building starts with knowing where it is.
- Visual localization must remain robust under crowding, lighting change, and environmental change.
- It is a core capability in the collected NAVER LABS spatial AI narrative.
