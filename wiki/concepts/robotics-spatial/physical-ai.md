# Physical AI

## Summary

Physical AI is AI that acts in, adapts to, or coordinates with the physical world. In this wiki, it covers robots, building infrastructure, spatial intelligence, human-robot interaction, and physical-world service workflows.

## Why It Matters

The move from screen-based AI to physical-world AI creates new UX and safety questions: where the robot is, what it can perceive, whether people accept its behavior, and how buildings support it.

## Key Claims

- Physical AI needs both spatial understanding and socially acceptable action.
- Buildings can become AI/robotics testbeds when they produce operational data.
- Robot UX depends on infrastructure as much as robot capability.
- Physical AI also depends on hardware product development, supply chains, manufacturability, and embodied safety.
