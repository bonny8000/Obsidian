# Dexterous Manipulation

## Summary

Dexterous manipulation is robot manipulation requiring fine-grained, high-degree-of-freedom control, often involving robot hands and complex contact-rich interactions.

## Why It Matters

Human hands are highly capable demonstration sources, but transferring that skill to robot hands requires solving embodiment, visual, and action-space mismatches.

## Key Claims

- Human hand behavior can be a valuable source of dexterous skill data.
- Hardware interfaces and software adaptation can bridge human and robot hand differences.
- Large-scale egocentric human video is being explored as a motor prior for dexterous robot policies.
