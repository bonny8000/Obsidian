# Spatial AI

## Summary

Spatial AI is AI that understands, models, localizes within, and reasons about physical space. In the collected NAVER LABS sources, it includes 3D reconstruction, digital twins, visual localization, and human-aware spatial understanding.

## Why It Matters

Robots and city-scale AI systems need spatial representations before they can navigate, interact, or provide services in real buildings and urban environments.

## Key Claims

- AI needs digital representations of physical spaces to operate beyond screens.
- Spatial AI connects mapping, localization, 3D reconstruction, and robotics.
- Real-world testbeds can provide data for improving spatial intelligence.
- AR/VR investment can transfer spatial tracking, SLAM, and depth-sensing techniques into robotics.
