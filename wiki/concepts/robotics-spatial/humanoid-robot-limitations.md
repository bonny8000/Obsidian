# Humanoid Robot Limitations

## Summary

Humanoid robot limitations are the current blockers that keep human-shaped robots from safe, reliable, large-scale deployment around people.

## Why It Matters

Humanoid robots are visually compelling, but the podcast frames them as advanced prototypes rather than mass-ready products. The limiting factors include safety, reliability, manufacturability, yield, cost, and supply chain maturity.

## Key Claims

- A strong robot near humans creates safety risk if mass, speed, or impact energy are not controlled.
- Softer, lighter, more compliant designs can reduce harm risk.
- Mass deployment requires robots to work reliably with little repair or human intervention.
- Manufacturing scale and supply chain constraints may be as important as model intelligence.
