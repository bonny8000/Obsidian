# Socially Aware Navigation

## Summary

Socially aware navigation is robot movement planning that accounts for human comfort, personal space, norms, intent, and acceptance, not only collision avoidance.

## Why It Matters

In shared buildings, a robot can be physically safe yet socially disruptive. Good navigation requires deciding when to move, wait, yield, or reroute.

## Key Claims

- Crowding and human acceptance are planning variables.
- Waiting can be the better UX choice even when a route is physically possible.
- Social navigation belongs in the same system as localization and spatial AI.
