# AI Hardware Boom

## Summary

The AI hardware boom is the emerging push toward devices, robotics, manufacturing, sensing, and physical-world systems as AI capability expands beyond software interfaces.

## Why It Matters

If software AI work eventually saturates behind-the-keyboard tasks, product opportunity may shift toward the physical world: robots, AR glasses, industrial systems, drones, manufacturing, and embodied interfaces.

## Key Claims

- Physical-world AI requires different constraints than software AI: components, manufacturing, supply chains, reliability, and safety.
- Hardware becomes attractive when AI labs and startups look for new frontiers beyond digital automation.
- Interview claims about market timing and scale should be treated as perspective, not settled forecast.
