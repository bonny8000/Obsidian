# Hardware Team Hiring

## Summary

Hardware team hiring is the practice of building teams that can design, validate, manufacture, and ship physical products under real-world constraints.

## Why It Matters

AI companies moving into devices and robotics need different talent than pure software teams. Hardware teams need systems thinking, reliability discipline, supply-chain awareness, manufacturing experience, and product taste.

## Key Claims

- Hardware expertise is becoming more valuable as AI moves toward physical systems.
- Strong hardware teams understand interdependent decisions and risk.
