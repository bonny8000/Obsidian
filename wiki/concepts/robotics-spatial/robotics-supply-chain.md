# Robotics Supply Chain

## Summary

The robotics supply chain includes the materials, components, manufacturing processes, and assembly expertise needed to build robots at scale.

## Why It Matters

Robotics deployment is limited not only by AI models. Physical robots require magnets, actuators, batteries, sensors, machined parts, subassemblies, and manufacturing capacity.

## Key Claims

- Magnets and actuators are foundational components for many robots and drones.
- Supply chains for robotics components have deep dependencies across countries and manufacturing ecosystems.
- Reindustrialization and resilient supply chains are strategic themes in the interview, but require external verification and policy context.
