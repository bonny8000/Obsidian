# Antigravity

## Summary

**Antigravity** (announced as "Antigravity 2.0" at Google I/O 2026) is Google's **agent development platform** — the build-side tooling for creating and deploying Gemini-powered agents, sitting alongside the Gemini 3.5 model family and the Gemini Spark assistant in Google's full-stack agentic strategy.

## Why It Matters

Frontier-model strategy increasingly ships with a *developer platform* for building agents on top of it (the model + the harness/IDE for agents). Antigravity is Google's entry in that category, comparable in role to other [[concepts/infrastructure-dev/enterprise-ai-agent-platform|enterprise agent platforms]] — relevant to how teams will actually build [[concepts/ai-agents/agentic-ai|agentic]] software.

## Key Claims

- Announced at Google I/O 2026 as an **agent development platform** ("Antigravity 2.0") within Google's full-stack (8th-gen TPUs + Gemini 3.5 + Spark) agentic push.
