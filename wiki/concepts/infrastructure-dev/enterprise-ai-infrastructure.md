# Enterprise AI Infrastructure

## Summary

Enterprise AI infrastructure is the organizational and technical layer for running AI systems with governance, security, identity, model access, logging, cost controls, and integration with business systems.

## Why It Matters

AI agents become operational systems when they can access enterprise data and tools. That makes infrastructure choices part of UX, design operations, and risk management.

## Key Claims

- Agentic AI adoption depends on infrastructure, not only model capability.
- Cloud platforms are positioning themselves as governance and deployment layers for enterprise AI.
- Infrastructure choices affect which AI workflows design teams can safely use.
