# AI as Infrastructure

## Summary

AI as Infrastructure is the view that AI systems become underlying social, urban, organizational, or service infrastructure rather than isolated features inside apps.

## Why It Matters

This framing changes the design question. Instead of asking what a model can do alone, the system designer asks how AI connects to people, institutions, buildings, robots, rights, and operational processes.

## Key Claims

- AI infrastructure includes social trust, service design, and deployment context, not only model capability.
- City-scale AI needs continuity between digital systems and physical environments.
- Digital sovereignty and public benefit are part of the infrastructure framing in the NAVER source.
