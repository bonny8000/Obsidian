# On-premise AI

> [!abstract] Summary
> AI systems that are deployed and operated within an organization's own local data centers or physical hardware, rather than in the public cloud.

> [!important] Why it Matters
> Crucial for security, data sovereignty, and latency-sensitive applications where cloud processing is not viable or compliant.

## 📝 Key Claims
- Allows organizations to maintain full control over their sensitive data.
- Reduces dependency on external cloud providers.
