# Graph-based Retrospection

## Summary
A method of looking back at past events, decisions, or projects by navigating the link relationships (graph) in a structured knowledge base.

## Why it Matters
It overcomes the limitations of human memory by allowing one to instantly see all connections (e.g., "What projects did I do at Company X that involved Technology Y?").

## Key Claims
- Structured links enable more accurate recall than free-form notes.
- Graph traversal helps identify patterns in career or personal development that might otherwise be missed.
