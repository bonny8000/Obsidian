# Edge AI

> [!abstract] Summary
> The deployment of AI models directly on local devices (sensors, cameras, robots) to process data at the source without sending it to a central cloud.

> [!important] Why it Matters
> Enables real-time responsiveness and offline functionality, which is critical for robotics and spatial AI applications.

## ?? Key Claims
- Minimizes latency by processing data locally.
- Enhances privacy by keeping data on the device.
