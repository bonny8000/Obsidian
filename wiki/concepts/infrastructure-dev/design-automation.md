# Design Automation

## Summary

Design automation uses AI agents and generative tools to reduce repetitive design work: research synthesis, layout exploration, HTML mockups, asset generation, and design-system-aware implementation.

## Why It Matters

The strongest use case is not replacing design judgment. It is moving the designer's work upward toward framing problems, steering outputs, reviewing quality, and preserving reusable patterns.

## Key Claims

- AI design workflows are more reliable when they can read existing design systems and codebases.
- Automation should preserve designer review and taste as control points.
- Reusable automation depends on clear instructions and documented workflows.
