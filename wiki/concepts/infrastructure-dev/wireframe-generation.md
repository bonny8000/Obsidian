# Wireframe Generation

## Summary

Wireframe generation uses AI or structured design tooling to turn requirements, flows, or product ideas into low-fidelity screen structures.

## Why It Matters

Wireframes help teams validate structure before investing in detailed visual design. AI generation can speed this up, but the result still needs information architecture and usability review.

## Key Claims

- Wireframes can be generated from upstream planning artifacts.
- Generated wireframes should be treated as drafts for review, not final UI.
- Linking wireframes to requirements improves traceability.
