# Agentic Engineering

## Summary
Agentic Engineering is an emerging discipline that focuses on building, managing, and optimizing software systems where autonomous AI agents perform a significant portion of the engineering work (coding, testing, deployment, and monitoring).

## Key Primitives
- **Agent Orchestration:** Managing multiple specialized agents to achieve complex goals.
- **Verification Loops:** Automated systems for checking agent output against requirements and standards.
- **Context Management:** Providing agents with the right amount of codebase and project context to be effective.

## Why it matters
It represents a shift from "human-led, AI-assisted" to "agent-led, human-governed" engineering. This shift significantly increases development velocity but requires new tools and architectures for safety, consistency, and reliability.
