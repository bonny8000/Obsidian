# Design Review Automation

## Summary

Design review automation uses tools, agents, screenshots, browser automation, or rule checks to compare implemented UI against design intent and quality criteria.

## Why It Matters

As AI generates more UI, automated review becomes necessary to catch layout, spacing, typography, color, and interaction mismatches before they become product defects.

## Key Claims

- Browser screenshots can be compared against design references.
- Automated review should augment, not replace, designer judgment.
- Review rules need design-system and product-context awareness.
