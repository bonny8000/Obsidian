# Object-Graph Mapping

## Summary
The process of mapping discrete data entities (Objects) and their interconnections (Links) into a coherent graph structure.

## Why it Matters
It is the technical foundation for [[concepts/infrastructure-dev/graph-based-retrospection|Graph-based Retrospection]] and [[concepts/infrastructure-dev/deterministic-ui|Deterministic UI]].

## Key Claims
- Data points are nodes, and relationships are edges.
- This mapping allows for complex queries across the entire information vault.
