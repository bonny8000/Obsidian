# Enterprise AI Agent Platform

## Summary

An enterprise AI agent platform provides shared infrastructure for building, deploying, governing, monitoring, and integrating AI agents across an organization.

## Why It Matters

Agent workflows become higher-risk in enterprise contexts because they touch data, tools, permissions, and accountability. Platforms promise shared controls for memory, identity, logging, governance, and deployment.

## Key Claims

- Enterprise agent platforms are framed as a move from experimentation to production operations.
- Design teams may use these platforms to connect research, feedback, design systems, and implementation workflows.
- Platform claims require verification against official vendor documentation.
