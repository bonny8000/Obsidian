# Knowledge Linting

## Summary

Knowledge linting is a maintenance pass over the wiki. It looks for broken links, orphan pages, duplicated concepts, unsourced claims, outdated information, and contradictions.

## Typical Checks

- Broken Obsidian links
- Orphaned concept pages
- Missing source references
- Conflicting claims
- Stale or superseded claims
- Recurring terms without concept pages
