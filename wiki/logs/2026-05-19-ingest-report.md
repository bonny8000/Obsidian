## Summary of Changes
Ingested three new sources covering AI persona replication (LinkedIn style), automated UX writing at scale (Rapport Labs), and the future of Agentic AI UX (pxd).

Key takeaways:
- **Stylistic Translation:** AI is moving toward "vibe-to-vibe" translation, allowing for social context adaptation.
- **Workflow Integration:** For AI-assisted writing (like UX copy) to work, it must be embedded in the designer's primary tool (Figma) and backed by a case database.
- **Agentic Shift:** UX is evolving from UI screens to "Harness Engineering"—designing the environments where agents operate autonomously.
- **GEO:** Brand visibility now requires optimizing for Generative Engines as AI agents become the primary way users find information.
