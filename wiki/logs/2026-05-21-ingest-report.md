## Summary of Changes
Ingested a deep-dive article on applying enterprise data modeling principles (Palantir Foundry) to a personal Obsidian vault.

Key takeaways:
- **Ontology is Infrastructure:** Personal vaults benefit from formal object-link schemas, making them queryable and durable for AI-assisted reflection.
- **Deterministic UI:** Portfolios and digital twins should be viewed as data-driven projections where the UI is a function of the underlying graph.
- **Precision PKM:** Moving beyond tables (Notion) and flat notes to a graph-based "Object Backend" allows for systematic retrospection.
