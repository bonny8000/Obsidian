# How will human-in-the-loop (HITL) dynamics evolve as agents become more autonomous?

## Short Answer
As agents become more autonomous, HITL shifts from approving individual steps to governing intent, reviewing artifacts, and intervening on exceptions. The Agentic AI sources describe this as a transition from "execution-oriented" work to "decision-oriented" governance—humans set strategy and review outcomes, while agents handle multi-step execution. The Cat Wu source adds that reliable automation still requires human sign-off until it approaches full trustworthiness.

- [[concepts/ai-agents/agentic-ai|Agentic AI]] — "Agentic AI enables a shift from 'execution-oriented' work to 'decision-oriented' governance."
- [[concepts/ai-agents/agentic-work-automation|Agentic Work Automation]] — "90-95% reliable automation may still require too much manual review. The goal is to push workflows toward trustable, repeatable execution with clear verification."
