# Which AI PM skills should be practiced first for Bonny's workflow: eval writing, automation design, or product strategy?

## Short Answer
Eval writing should come first because it is the concrete skill that makes the other two testable. Without evals, automation design has no quality signal and product strategy lacks behavioral definitions. The Cat Wu source explicitly frames evals as a foundational PM skill for defining what good AI behavior looks like.

## Evidence
- [[concepts/ai-agents/product-evals|Product Evals]] ??"PMs do not always need hundreds of evals; a small set of strong evals can be useful. Evals are most valuable when a feature needs clearer behavioral definition."
- [[concepts/product-management/ai-pm-skills|AI PM Skills]] ??"Strong AI PMs understand the current model's limits, not just the future AGI vision. PMs should be able to define what successful behavior looks like through examples and evals."
- [[sources/lennys-podcast-cat-wu-ai-pm-claude-code|Lenny's Podcast: Cat Wu on AI-Native PM]] ??"Focused evals help define success for model behavior and product quality."
