# What governance model should accompany AI treated as infrastructure?

## Short Answer
AI as infrastructure requires a governance model that covers: (1) public benefit and digital sovereignty?ho owns and controls the infrastructure; (2) service continuity and fault accountability?ho is responsible when AI-enabled infrastructure fails; (3) interoperability standards?ow different AI infrastructure components connect safely; and (4) community oversight?ow affected people can audit and challenge AI infrastructure decisions. The NAVER LABS sources frame these as social and institutional questions, not only technical ones.
