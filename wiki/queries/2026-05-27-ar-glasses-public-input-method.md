# What input method will make AR glasses practical in public settings?

## Short Answer
The wiki evidence points to two candidate input methods: (1) subtle gesture input using hand or finger micro-movements that are socially invisible in public; and (2) sub-vocal or bone-conduction voice input that does not require audible speech. Both avoid the social friction of visible talking-to-glasses or large hand gestures. The Kalinowski source frames the input problem as one of the key remaining blockers alongside display and cost.
