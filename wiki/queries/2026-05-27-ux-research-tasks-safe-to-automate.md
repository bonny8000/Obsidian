# Which UX research tasks are safe to automate, and which require human interpretation?

## Short Answer
Tasks that are safe to automate include: scheduling and note transcription, quantitative data collection (unmoderated tests, task-success/time metrics), affinity diagram sorting, literature summaries, and draft report generation. Tasks that require human interpretation include: reflexive thematic analysis, qualitative meaning-making, participant-relationship management, and any analysis that is part of the method itself (where the researcher's positionality and reflexivity are the instrument).
