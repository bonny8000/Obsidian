# What documentation should be required when AI is used in UX research analysis?

## Short Answer
Required documentation should include: (1) a methods appendix identifying each analysis step and whether it was AI-assisted or human-led; (2) the validation check applied to AI-generated outputs (e.g., inter-rater reliability, human review pass, any-2 agreement for usability findings); (3) the model and prompt used if reproducibility matters; and (4) a statement of how AI use affected or did not affect the validity of the conclusions.
