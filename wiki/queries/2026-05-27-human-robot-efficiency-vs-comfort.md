# What interaction rules should robots follow when their efficiency conflicts with human comfort?

## Short Answer
When efficiency conflicts with human comfort, robots should default to human comfort in public and shared spaces. The NAVER LABS elevator research shows that people prefer robots that give up and wait over robots that force entry. The operating rule is: if boarding or proceeding would require a person to adjust position, yield and wait for the next opportunity. Task efficiency is a secondary objective in human-occupied spaces.
