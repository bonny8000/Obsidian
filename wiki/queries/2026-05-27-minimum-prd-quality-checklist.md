# What should Bonny's minimum PRD quality checklist include before moving into design or code?

## Short Answer
The minimum PRD quality checklist before advancing to design or code: (1) target user and primary use case are stated; (2) success criteria are specific and measurable; (3) scope exclusions are explicit (what is not in scope); (4) key assumptions are listed and can be validated; (5) the PRD is linked to or consistent with upstream research, stakeholder decisions, or constraints; and (6) the wireframe or flow is ready as a structural companion.
