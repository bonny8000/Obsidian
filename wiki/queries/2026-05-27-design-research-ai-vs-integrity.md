# Which design research deliverables can be AI-assisted without weakening methodological integrity?

## Short Answer
AI assistance is safe for: literature and secondary research summaries, competitive benchmarking, quantitative data analysis and charting, draft interview screeners and discussion guides, note transcription, and synthesis documents where a human researcher validates each claim. AI assistance weakens integrity when it replaces reflexive or situated interpretive work, manufactures participant quotes or insights not grounded in evidence, or generates conclusions without human review.
