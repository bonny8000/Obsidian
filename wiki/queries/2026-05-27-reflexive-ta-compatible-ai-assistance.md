# What forms of AI assistance, if any, are compatible with reflexive thematic analysis?

## Short Answer
AI can legitimately assist with pre-interpretive tasks in RTA: transcription, formatting, literature search, and generating initial candidate codes as prompts for human reflection. What AI cannot replace is the reflexive work itself?he researcher's situated meaning-making, positionality disclosure, and iterative interpretation. Framing AI output as a stimulus for human reflection rather than a substitute for it is the key boundary.
