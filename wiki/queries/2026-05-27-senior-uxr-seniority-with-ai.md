# How will the definition of "Seniority" change as AI automates more of the foundational research tasks?

## Short Answer
Seniority will shift away from volume-of-experience markers (number of sessions conducted, studies completed) toward judgment markers: knowing when and how to use AI tools appropriately, stakeholder navigation, research strategy, and the ability to catch AI-generated findings that are unreliable. The technical skill floor may lower, but the interpretive, ethical, and organizational judgment ceiling will become the actual differentiator.
