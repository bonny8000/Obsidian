# How should robots communicate a decision to wait or yield?

## Short Answer
Robots should communicate a wait or yield decision through: (1) a clear physical stop or backing gesture that makes the decision legible; (2) an audio or visual signal (light, brief sound, or screen message) that indicates the robot is intentionally waiting rather than malfunctioning; and (3) positioning away from the path so people are not blocked by a stationary robot. The goal is to make the robot's intent readable without requiring people to engage with it.
