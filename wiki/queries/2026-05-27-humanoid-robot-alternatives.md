# Which non-humanoid forms solve common robot use cases more safely or cheaply?

## Short Answer
The wiki evidence identifies four non-humanoid forms that are already more practical for specific use cases: (1) wheeled/mobile robots for indoor delivery (lower mass, simpler safety profile than bipeds); (2) robotic arms on fixed bases for manipulation tasks in structured environments; (3) drones for outdoor delivery and inspection; and (4) purpose-built modular robots for specific industrial tasks. These avoid humanoid complexity while solving the core tasks without the safety and cost burden of a human-shaped body.
