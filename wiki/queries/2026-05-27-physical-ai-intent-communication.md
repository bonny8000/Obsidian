# How should physical AI systems expose their intent to nearby people?

## Short Answer
Physical AI systems should expose intent through: (1) legible motion?ovements that clearly signal direction, speed, and stopping point before reaching a person's space; (2) ambient signaling?ights, sounds, or screen displays that communicate state (active, waiting, picking up); (3) predictable behavior patterns?onsistent rules people can learn, not random or erratic changes; and (4) graceful failure?hen a task cannot be completed, stop visibly rather than making unpredictable compensating moves.
