# What validity checks should accompany AI-generated usability issue lists?

## Short Answer
Three validity checks should accompany AI-generated usability issue lists: (1) ground each issue in participant evidence (observed behavior, quote, or timestamp); (2) have a human expert review the list for false positives and missed high-severity issues; and (3) compare the AI list against a human-generated list from the same session to estimate convergent validity. Issues that survive all three have the strongest claim to validity.
