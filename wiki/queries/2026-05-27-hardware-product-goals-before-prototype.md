# How should AI hardware teams define measurable goals before prototype builds?

## Short Answer
Before a prototype build, AI hardware teams should define measurable goals in six categories: (1) target performance metrics (speed, accuracy, latency); (2) physical constraints (weight, size, power); (3) manufacturing targets (yield rate, BOM cost, assembly complexity); (4) reliability threshold (failure rate at X hours of operation); (5) safety envelope (force limits, operating speeds, emergency stop response time); and (6) prioritized risk list?hich of these is hardest to achieve and must be designed for first.
