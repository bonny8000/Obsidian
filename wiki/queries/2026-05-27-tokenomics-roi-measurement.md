# How will companies measure ROI in a "Tokenomics" era?

## Short Answer
In a tokenomics era, ROI is measured by comparing token cost to the value of cognitive labor displaced or accelerated. The primary metrics will be: cost-per-completed-task (tokens consumed divided by business outcome), token efficiency ratio (output value per token), and the ratio of automated to human labor hours for equivalent work. Companies that optimize their operating model around AI agents will measure ROI at the workflow level, not the individual query level.
