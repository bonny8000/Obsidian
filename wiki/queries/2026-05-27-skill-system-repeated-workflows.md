# Which repeated Bonny workflows deserve dedicated skills?

## Short Answer
Based on the wiki's current structure, four workflows recur enough to warrant dedicated skills: (1) wiki ingest (adding a new source and creating/updating concept pages); (2) knowledge linting (checking for broken links, low-confidence pages, and orphan concepts); (3) query answering (finding and synthesizing evidence for an open question); and (4) design research synthesis (summarizing multiple sources into a structured research brief). These map to the three main LLM Wiki operations plus the design research workflow that appears across multiple sources.
