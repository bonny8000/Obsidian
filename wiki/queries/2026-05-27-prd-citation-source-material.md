# How should AI-generated PRDs cite source material and stakeholder decisions?

## Short Answer
AI-generated PRDs should include an explicit "Sources" section linking to the research reports, stakeholder meetings, user feedback, or constraints that informed each major decision. Specific claims?specially user needs, acceptance criteria, and scope exclusions?hould be traceable to a named source. Stakeholder decisions should be attributed by decision date and approver, not buried in the AI-generated narrative.
