# What hardware quality principles transfer from laptops/headsets to home robots?

## Short Answer
Four principles transfer: (1) attention to detail at every level of the product, including parts users never see?eliability and durability emerge from this discipline; (2) cost as a design strategy?esigning for a target price point forces better system trade-offs; (3) reliability-first component selection?rioritizing components with known failure rates over novel parts; and (4) user-facing components get the most iteration cycles?hatever the robot touches or presents to the user must earn extra design investment.
