# Which parts of Bonny's workflow should remain specialist-led versus AI-augmented generalist work?

## Short Answer
Specialist-led work: UX research design (study planning, participant recruitment, analysis of sensitive qualitative data), design system architecture decisions, and stakeholder alignment on product strategy. AI-augmented generalist work: secondary research synthesis, first-draft documentation, prototype generation from clear briefs, and quantitative data processing. The specialist work requires deep method knowledge and accountability; the generalist work benefits from AI speed without sacrificing quality.
