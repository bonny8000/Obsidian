# Which UX research methods are compatible with AI assistance and which are not?

## Short Answer
Compatible with AI assistance: usability testing analysis (with reliability checks), survey coding and quantitative analysis, secondary research, journey map drafting from structured data, heuristic checklist application, and unmoderated test data processing. Not compatible (or only compatible at pre-interpretive stages): reflexive thematic analysis, grounded theory, phenomenology, and any method where researcher positionality is part of the analysis instrument.
