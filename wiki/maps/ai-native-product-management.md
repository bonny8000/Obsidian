# AI-Native Product Management

## Core Idea

This cluster tracks how product work changes when LLMs and agents become part of the product itself and the team's internal operating system. The main pattern is not simply "ship faster"; it is to combine high shipping velocity with product taste, evals, onboarding, and reliable agentic automation.

## Working Interpretation

The useful takeaway for Bonny's LLM Wiki is operational: treat ingest, synthesis, linking, and maintenance as product workflows. Define the user task, keep source traceability, run link checks, collect feedback, and turn repeated corrections into durable rules or evals.
