# AI Hardware and Physical AI

## Core Idea

This cluster connects AI's movement into the physical world with the hardware disciplines needed to make that movement real: product development, supply chains, embodied safety, spatial perception, and human-robot interaction.

## Working Interpretation

The useful synthesis is that physical AI is not only a model problem. It is a product and systems problem: the robot or device has to be manufactured, supplied, verified, made safe, made socially acceptable, and connected to spatial understanding.
