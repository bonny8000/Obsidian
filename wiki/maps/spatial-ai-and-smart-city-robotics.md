# Spatial AI and Smart City Robotics

## Core Idea

This cluster connects AI to physical space: mapping environments, localizing robots, modeling cities, coordinating infrastructure, and designing human-acceptable robot behavior.

## Working Interpretation

The design issue is not only whether AI or robots can act in a space. It is whether the space, infrastructure, robot behavior, hardware constraints, supply chains, and human expectations form a coherent service system.
