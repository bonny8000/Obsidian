# Method: MaxDiff Prioritization

## Purpose

MaxDiff helps teams estimate relative preference or importance by asking participants to choose the most and least important items from repeated sets.

## Use When

- The team needs sharper prioritization than simple rating scales.
- Items are comparable and understandable to participants.
- The output will inform product, messaging, or roadmap choices.

## Avoid When

- The candidate item list is not mature.
- Items combine multiple ideas and create ambiguous tradeoffs.
- The team needs deep explanation before ranking.

## Inputs

- Candidate item list.
- Sampling plan.
- Experimental design or survey setup.
- Analysis plan for scores and segments.

## Procedure

1. Clean and normalize item wording.
2. Validate that participants understand each item.
3. Run the MaxDiff task with balanced item exposure.
4. Estimate item preference or importance scores.
5. Interpret scores with qualitative context where possible.

## Outputs

- Ranked item list.
- Relative score gaps.
- Segment-level differences if the sample supports them.
- Decision recommendation for what to emphasize, cut, or test next.

## Quality Bar

- Avoid treating small score differences as decisive without uncertainty.
- Keep item wording stable from pilot to final run.
- Combine with qualitative evidence when prioritizing complex needs.

## LLM Assistance

- **Safe uses:** cleaning item wording, flagging overlap, drafting interpretation.
- **Risky uses:** generating final item sets without stakeholder or participant validation.
- **Verification required:** design balance, sample assumptions, and score interpretation.
