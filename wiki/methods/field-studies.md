# Method: Field Studies

## Purpose

Observe people in their real environment doing real work, to surface context, constraints, and workarounds that never appear in interviews or lab sessions.

## Use When

- The environment shapes behavior: physical space, interruptions, tools-in-combination, social dynamics.
- Users have normalized their workarounds and cannot articulate them.
- The team needs grounded workflow models before designing.

## Avoid When

- Access, privacy, or safety constraints make genuine observation impossible.
- The question is a comparative evaluation of design options (use [[methods/usability-testing|Usability Testing]]).
- Observer presence would change the behavior beyond interpretability.

## Inputs

- Site access agreements and consent covering observation and capture.
- A focus frame: which activities, roles, and artifacts to attend to.
- Observation protocol balancing pure observation with contextual-inquiry questioning.

## Procedure

1. Define the focus frame and what would count as a surprising observation.
2. Observe first; interrupt with questions only at natural pauses (master–apprentice stance).
3. Capture artifacts: photos of workspaces, copies of cheat sheets, tool inventories.
4. Debrief within 24 hours per visit; write observations before interpretations.
5. Build workflow models and consolidate across sites.

## Outputs

- Observation records separated from interpretation.
- Workflow and environment models.
- Workaround and artifact inventory.
- Opportunity areas tied to observed friction.

## Quality Bar

- Notes distinguish what was seen from what was inferred.
- Findings cite specific observed instances, not impressions.
- Sites and roles sampled deliberately, with coverage limits stated.

## LLM Assistance

- **Safe uses:** structuring raw field notes, drafting workflow models for researcher correction.
- **Risky uses:** generating "typical environment" descriptions; smoothing over site-to-site variation.
- **Verification required:** models reviewed against photos and notes from each site.
