# Decision: Evaluation UX [A,I]

## Context & Background
Extracted from AICS 21 Meeting (2026-05-18). This meeting focused on evaluating the UX of AI tools and establishing standard operating procedures (SOPs) for the FrontEnd team.

## Options Considered
1. Phase-based narrative for presentations (Phase 1, 2, 3).
2. Workflow timeline narrative.

## Decision Made
For external presentations, the narrative should follow a **workflow timeline** rather than arbitrary "Phase 1/2/3" designations. This makes the progression of the AI Agent's capabilities more understandable to stakeholders.

## Evidence & Justification
- The current demo run focuses on "ROG Wallpaper."
- There is a cross-departmental risk: if the FrontEnd team only relies on wireframes or Figma to run AI processes, they will lack crucial UX specifications and interactive rules.
- Grounded in: [[concepts/product-management/stakeholder-management|Stakeholder Management]]
- Supported by: [[concepts/infrastructure-dev/design-system-implementation|Design System Implementation]]

## Consequences & Next Steps
Ensure all upcoming presentations adopt the workflow timeline format. The UX team must proactively provide UX specs to the FE team, not just Figma files, to mitigate the identified risk.
