## Key Decisions
- **Decision (Agent Quadrants):** The research is structured around a "Full Landscape Map" (全景地圖) dividing agents by platform boundary:
  - **Inside Platform:** Chatbot Agent Service, Browser Integration, Financial Payment Experience, Onsite Experience Agent.
  - **Outside Platform:** ACP (Brand Embedded AI Platform), UCP (Google Universal Commerce Protocol), Comet (autonomous cross-site price comparison/login), Payment Network Agents (Visa/Mastercard).
