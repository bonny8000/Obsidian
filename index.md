## Recent Ingests

- **The response gap, crawl consent, and measurement under adaptation (2026-08-04, 8 sources):** two same-day-apart sources converge on a problem neither knew existed; two same-batch sources contradict each other outright.
  - [[wiki/sources/evan-moon-identity-in-programming|Evan Moon: Why identity is the hardest problem in programming]] (**0.78, highest in the batch**, `coverage: full`) — identity is a *declared contract*, not a property of data, and *"bugs go off not when a contract is broken, but at the boundary where contracts meet."* Three sameness dimensions (reference / structural / domain), the equivalence axioms, and why violations fail silently. New concept: [[wiki/concepts/infrastructure-dev/identity-contract|Identity Contract]]. **The most valuable unexplored link in this ingest:** agent memory is an undeclared identity contract, and this predicts [[wiki/concepts/ai-agents/memory-contamination|memory contamination]]'s exact failure mode.
  - [[wiki/sources/saeidehbakhshi-usability-metrics-static-product|Bakhshi: Usability metrics assume the product stays still]] (0.72, `coverage: full`) — *"different users are using different versions of the product,"* so *"the number may be precise while the underlying construct it is measuring is not the same thing."* Inverts the field's most-used metric: **less effort is ambiguous, not good.** New concepts: [[wiki/concepts/ux-research/measurement-under-adaptation|Measurement Under Adaptation]], [[wiki/concepts/ux-research/steerability|Steerability]].
  - [[wiki/sources/nngroup-ux-context-design|Alicea / NN/g: UX-Context Design]] (0.66, `coverage: full`) — names what this vault has been doing: research output curated as **machine context** rather than as persuasion. *"Its success is measured by whether AI output improves, not by whether stakeholders are convinced."* New concept: [[wiki/concepts/ux-research/ux-context-design|UX-Context Design]]. **Scored down hard for having no evidence at all** behind its central claim, from the institution best placed to supply it.
  - [[wiki/sources/cloudflare-responsible-ai-bot-principles|Cloudflare: Responsible AI Bot Principles]] (0.64, `coverage: full`) — five principles, of which one is novel: **declare a single purpose** (search / AI-input / training) because bundling forces *"an impossible choice onto website owners."* New concept: [[wiki/concepts/infrastructure-dev/ai-crawler-governance|AI Crawler Governance]]. Vendor advocacy over real RFCs; **Web Bot Auth is the load-bearing dependency and it is still a draft.**
  - [[wiki/sources/toyota-voice-interaction-humanoid-robots|Toyota FRC: Voice interaction with humanoid robots]] (0.62, `coverage: full`) — three latency techniques described in reimplementable detail (parallel speculative execution, a **main > clarification > filler** priority ladder, and "think while listening"), plus the persona decision to encode *philosophy rather than transcripts*. Honest close: they still *"cannot get them to consistently respond within one second."* **Zero measurement of anything.**
  - [[wiki/sources/google-search-io-2026-agents|Google: Search at I/O 2026]] (0.55, `coverage: full`) — **Generative UI free to all Search users**; 24/7 Information Agents; agentic booking extended to **phoning businesses on the user's behalf**. The rollout gating is the strategy statement: *generated surfaces free, delegated action paid.*
  - [[wiki/sources/boongranii-cursor-pointer-debate|Boongranii: Should clickable elements use cursor: pointer?]] (0.60, `coverage: full`) — the vault's only worked case of **specification versus twenty-year learned convention.** New concept: [[wiki/concepts/ux-research/perceived-affordance|Perceived Affordance]].
  - [[wiki/sources/paxton-yao-voice-ai-thinking-state|Yao: Voice AI gave designers a new state to show — thinking]] (0.48) — *"gap is a new state that didn't exist before."* Two states on desktop, five in a car; state count follows viewing context. New concept: [[wiki/concepts/agent-experience/system-state-signaling|System State Signaling]].
  - **Memo 1 — the convergence:** [[wiki/analyses/2026-08-04-the-response-gap|The Response Gap]] plus the decision table [[wiki/comparisons/filling-the-response-gap|Filling the Response Gap]]. Toyota and Yao published a day apart, from robotics and automotive, solving the same LLM-latency problem with **speech and colour respectively**, neither aware of the other. The memo's contribution: **the gap is a turn-taking problem, not a loading state** — NOMI's "countdown" is a floor-holding signal and Toyota's fillers are the same move in speech. New concept: [[wiki/concepts/agent-experience/response-latency-masking|Response Latency Masking]].
  - **Memo 2 — the contradiction:** [[wiki/analyses/2026-08-04-crawl-consent-vs-answer-surfaces|Crawl Consent vs. Answer Surfaces]]. Cloudflare says declare one purpose and honour operator preferences; Google announces agents monitoring blogs and news 24/7 and a generated surface replacing the visit, with the publisher side unmentioned. **Recorded, not merged.** Also flags that Google publishes both *"optimise to be cited"* (GEO) and *"the answer is now a generated component."*
  - **A gap no concept covers yet:** agentic calling reaches a **third party with no interface, no disclosure, and no seat on the [[wiki/concepts/agent-experience/delegation-spectrum|delegation spectrum]].**
  - **Two URLs could not be captured** — `uibowl.io/self-contents/cmrmx3dsz…` and `…cmrlbsm8g…` (client-rendered SPA, no server-side content, domain blocked in the browser pane). Recorded as deferred in [[log|the operations log]]; no pages created.

- **Constraint architectures + reporting and modality (2026-07-31, 5 sources):** three sources converge on the same constraint principle; two pair off on a separate theme.
  - [[wiki/sources/karrot-kraft-design-system-agent|Karrot: Kraft]] (0.80, `coverage: full`) — a design system team's screen generator across three architectures (**admin → CLI → agent**), each transition documented with the wall that forced it.
